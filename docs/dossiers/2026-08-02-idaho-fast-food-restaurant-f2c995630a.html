Consensus Facts

  • A shooting occurred at an In-N-Out Burger restaurant in Twin Falls, southern Idaho, on Saturday afternoon (Aug. 1, 2026).
  • Three people were killed and seven more were injured, some critically (per Politico and Associated Press body text, both citing the Twin Falls city spokesman).
  • The suspected gunman was found dead near the restaurant; police were still working to determine the shooter's identity and motive.
  • Twin Falls Police Chief Matthew Hicks said in a news conference, 'We believe the threat to the community is over,' and described it as 'a very chaotic scene.' (Politico and ABC News)
  • Witness Lane Koehn, 34, described seeing a person with an AR-style rifle emerge from the drive-thru while a man with a pistol fired at the shooter, and saw an In-N-Out employee drag a wounded, uniformed woman across the parking lot. (Politico and ABC News)
  • Officials had not yet released victims' names as they were still notifying next of kin. (Politico and ABC News)
