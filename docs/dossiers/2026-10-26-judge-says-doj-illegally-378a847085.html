Consensus Facts

  • A federal judge ruled on Monday that the Justice Department's July 2025 firing of Maurene Comey, daughter of former FBI Director James Comey, was unlawful.
  • The judge is U.S. District Judge Jesse Furman, sitting in the Southern District of New York (Manhattan).
  • Furman ordered Maurene Comey be reinstated/retroactively reinstated with back pay from her July 16, 2025 termination through Dec. 20, 2025, when she accepted/began a new job.
  • The Justice Department said it disagrees with the decision and will appeal 'in due course.'
  • Comey's lawyers released a statement calling it a 'hard-earned victory' belonging 'to Ms. Comey, to all civil servants, and to all fighting for the rule of law.'
  • Comey's lawsuit (filed September 2025) argued she was fired because her father is James Comey and/or because of her perceived political affiliation and beliefs.
  • Maurene Comey worked on high-profile cases including the prosecutions of Jeffrey Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell, and Sean 'Diddy' Combs.
  • The firing was directed by then-Attorney General Pam Bondi, according to a DOJ court filing.
  • Furman rejected the DOJ's argument that the attorney general could fire prosecutors at will, saying it conflicts with nearly two centuries of Supreme Court precedent.
  • The ruling found the firing violated the constitutional separation of powers and Comey's Fifth Amendment due process rights.
  • James Comey, whom Trump fired as FBI director in 2017, is currently under a federal indictment over an Instagram post of seashells arranged as '86 47,' alleged to threaten Trump, and a prior indictment against him was dismissed.
  • Furman noted Comey received excellent performance reviews and high accolades from supervisors and peers.
