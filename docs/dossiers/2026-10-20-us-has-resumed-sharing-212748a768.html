  • Secretary of State Marco Rubio denied that any US support was deliberately withheld, saying it 'wasn't deliberate,' that nobody ordered Starlink speed slowed, and that it had been restored (CBS, NBC, Al Jazeera).
  • Trump publicly criticized Zelenskyy, accusing him of not wanting to end the war and saying 'he doesn't want to end this war, and probably never did,' and that Zelenskyy could have made a better deal two years ago (CBS, Al Jazeera, NBC).
  • Trump waived/eased sanctions on Russia, including on Russian diesel exports, in a bid to lower US energy/fuel prices (CBS, Al Jazeera, NBC).
  • Zelenskyy described the waiving of Russian diesel sanctions as a 'reward' for Putin that would embolden him (CBS, Al Jazeera, NBC).
  • Zelenskyy held or planned talks in Norway (Oslo) with US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, with Kushner participating remotely, alongside European leaders including the Norwegian PM and Finnish officials (Al Jazeera, NBC).
  • Ukraine and Russia continued trading long-range air strikes over the period, with civilian casualties reported on both sides (CBS, Al Jazeera, NBC).
  • Russia struck Ukrainian energy and civilian infrastructure, causing power outages (CBS, Al Jazeera, NBC).
