Consensus Facts

  • President Donald Trump hosted President Xi Jinping at the White House for a state visit, Xi's first visit to Washington in more than a decade.
  • The state dinner featured a large contingent of US tech and business leaders, including Apple's Tim Cook, Nvidia's Jensen Huang, OpenAI's Sam Altman, Amazon's Jeff Bezos, Alphabet's Sundar Pichai, Elon Musk, and Mark Zuckerberg.
  • Xi stated that both countries must ensure AI/its development remains under human control.
  • Trump delayed/held in abeyance a roughly $14bn arms package for Taiwan, which he had described as a 'very good negotiating chip' after his May visit to China.
  • Taiwan was a point of tension, with Xi urging Trump to take the 'correct position' / oppose 'Taiwan independence' on the self-governing island Beijing claims.
  • The visit was heavy on ceremony and symbolism, with no clear public indication that the closed-door talks produced breakthroughs on key issues (AI, trade, Taiwan).
  • First Lady Melania Trump planned the dinner menu, which included sesame-crusted sea bass, described as showcasing American ingredients with subtle Chinese influence.
