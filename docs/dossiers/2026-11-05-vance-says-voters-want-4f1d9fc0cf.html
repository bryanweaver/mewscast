Consensus Facts

  • Vice President JD Vance blamed Republicans' 2026 midterm losses on the war with Iran in a lengthy post on X, writing 'The reason we lost is the war. The president himself said so last night.'
  • Vance identified four reasons voters who backed Trump/Vance in 2024 voted for Democrats in 2026: ending the war with Iran, the cost of living and inflation, putting America first, and changing the U.S. relationship with Israel.

  • Vance cited an unnamed pollster/data analyst he respects whose analysis of voter priorities he described as accurate or 'prophetic.'
  • Vance argued the main problem was not Republican turnout but that many 2024 Trump/Vance voters voted for Democrats in 2026.
  • President Trump acknowledged the Iran war was a factor in the losses but defended his decision, saying he would have done the same again.
  • The U.S. war with Iran, launched in collaboration with Israel in late February 2026, drove up oil and gas prices, a key economic factor cited around the election.
  • Vance is described as a potential 2028 Republican presidential contender.
  • These were Vance's first public comments since the Democrats' midterm victory.
