Consensus Facts

  • An Amazon cargo plane overran a runway at Miami International Airport on Sunday, September 6, 2026, and crashed.
  • Five people were killed and five others were injured in the incident.
  • The aircraft was a Boeing 767 operated by 21 Air, a cargo carrier based in North Carolina.
  • The plane struck multiple vehicles on the ground after overrunning the runway and burst into flames.
  • At least three of the injured were in critical condition, and some victims were trapped inside or under vehicles.
  • The flight originated in San Juan, Puerto Rico (Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport) before landing in Miami.
  • The FAA temporarily grounded flights at the airport, and the NTSB is investigating the crash.
  • The crash occurred over Labor Day weekend, one of the busiest US travel periods of the year.
  • Miami-Dade County Mayor Daniella Levine Cava announced the casualty figures at a news conference.
  • The Amazon flight was designated as Prime Air Flight 7598 (also referenced as Amazon Air flight 7598).
