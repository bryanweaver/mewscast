Consensus Facts

  • A 14-year-old boy carried out a shooting at Debsirin Nonthaburi School in Bang Kruai district, Nonthaburi province, on the outskirts of Bangkok, Thailand, on Friday, August 7, 2026.
  • Before attacking the school, the teen fatally shot his grandparents at their home, where he lived.
  • The weapon used was a handgun that belonged to his grandfather.
  • The suspect died after turning the weapon on himself.
  • At least five people were killed at the school (initial reports of six, including students, were later revised down to five).
  • Roughly 23 to more than 30 people were injured, with about nine in critical condition (figures vary by outlet).
  • Police said the gunman fired at least 26 bullets and was found with 34 additional rounds of ammunition.
  • Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul commented publicly, expressing sorrow and indicating the attack appeared planned; he raised the prospect of tightening gun laws or school security.
  • Thailand has among the highest rates of civilian gun ownership in Southeast Asia — roughly 15 guns per 100 people / about 10 million firearms, per 2017 Small Arms Survey data — and the second-highest number of gun homicides in the region after the Philippines.
  • The attack is described as one of Thailand's deadliest school shootings/mass killings in recent years, following a February 2026 school shooting and a 2022 childcare/nursery massacre that killed dozens.
