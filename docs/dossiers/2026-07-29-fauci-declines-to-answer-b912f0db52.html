Consensus Facts

  • Dr. Anthony Fauci invoked his Fifth Amendment right against self-incrimination and declined to answer questions at a Senate committee hearing on Wednesday, July 29, 2026.
  • The hearing concerned the origins of the COVID-19 pandemic and Fauci's handling of it, held before the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee.
  • Republican Senator Rand Paul of Kentucky, who chairs the committee, subpoenaed Fauci to appear.
  • Days before the hearing, Paul released over 1,000 pages of Fauci's pandemic-era 'diary' or private writings, accusing him of covering up or misrepresenting COVID-19's origins.
  • Fauci accused Paul of being obsessed with getting him prosecuted or 'behind bars,' and noted he had previously testified about COVID under oath at prior congressional hearings.
  • Fauci stated it 'pains me to do so because of the respect I have for the legislative branch of government' but invoked the Fifth on the advice of his attorneys.
  • Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. referenced obtaining the diary entries from '11 separate servers.'
  • The current scientific consensus, as reported, is that COVID-19 likely originated naturally, while the lab-leak theory (favored by Paul and Trump) points to a Chinese lab in Wuhan.
