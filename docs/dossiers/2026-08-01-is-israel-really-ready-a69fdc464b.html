Consensus Facts

  • President Donald Trump announced a deal/roadmap under which Hamas would disarm and Israel would withdraw its forces from Gaza (reported in The Washington Post, Al Jazeera, CNBC, AP, and CNN bodies).
  • Hamas has said it will only disarm if Israel honors its commitments — ceasing attacks, withdrawing forces per the ceasefire, and allowing more humanitarian aid in (CNBC and CNN both quote Hamas official Ghazi Hamad; Washington Post also notes Hamas confirmation).
  • As of these reports, the Israeli government had not officially commented on the roadmap (stated in Washington Post, Al Jazeera, CNBC, AP, and CNN).
  • An unnamed Israeli official said there would be no Israeli withdrawal from the Gaza 'Yellow Line' until the 'complete/genuine disarmament' of Hamas (reported in Washington Post, Al Jazeera, and CNN).
  • Israel and Hamas signed a US-backed ceasefire that took effect in October, which called for gradual Israeli withdrawal, an international stabilization force, and decommissioning of Hamas weapons; implementation subsequently stalled (Washington Post and CNBC).
  • The war began with the Hamas-led attack on southern Israel on October 7, 2023, which killed around 1,200 people and took 251 hostages (Washington Post and Al Jazeera).
  • More than 73,000 Palestinians have been killed in Israel's offensive in Gaza (73,300 per Washington Post citing the Gaza Health Ministry; 'more than 73,000' per Al Jazeera).

  • The document establishes a phased, verification-contingent process (International Verification Committee certifies each phase before proceeding), supporting Trump's own 'as disarmament is completed' sequencing described in CNBC — but the text does not resolve the sequencing dispute at the heart of the Israel/Hamas standoff (who moves first), which each side interprets in its favor.
