Consensus Facts

  • On Tuesday, August 18, 2026, ABC and its parent company Disney filed a lawsuit against the FCC in federal court (DC District Court) alleging First Amendment violations.
  • The lawsuit targets the FCC's demand for early renewal of the broadcast licenses of the eight ABC-owned-and-operated TV stations, years ahead of their scheduled expiration.
  • The lawsuit's central allegation, quoted across outlets, is that the Administration 'has waged a retaliatory campaign against ABC for a single reason: it disapproves of what ABC broadcasts.'
  • The FCC cited Disney's diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) practices as the basis for its review, and had been examining alleged DEI discrimination for roughly a year (since 2025).
  • FCC Chairman Brendan Carr is a Trump ally/appointee and has been at the center of the administration's confrontation with ABC.
  • The early license-renewal order is described as unprecedented in the modern era — the FCC had not demanded early renewal in decades (BBC cites over 50 years).
  • The lawsuit asks the court for a temporary restraining order and/or injunction to halt the FCC's early license renewal proceedings.
  • The FCC responded via spokesperson/Carr that all broadcasters must operate in the public interest and that it will 'continue to follow the facts and law wherever they lead.'
  • The dispute is tied to renewed backlash against ABC late-night host Jimmy Kimmel, an outspoken Trump critic, and to the daytime talk show 'The View.'
  • Trump previously sued ABC/George Stephanopoulos for defamation over remarks about the E. Jean Carroll case, which ABC settled by paying approximately $15-16 million.
  • Disney CEO Josh D'Amaro, who took over from Bob Iger in March, defended ABC, saying the company is 'not going to be told how to run that side of our business.'
  • The lawsuit quotes Trump's own social media posts, including his call to strip or terminate ABC's licenses, as evidence of retaliation.
  • Carr had previously warned media companies over Kimmel that they could 'do this the easy way or the hard way'; Kimmel was suspended/pulled off air and returned days later after public backlash.
