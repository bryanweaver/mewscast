Consensus Facts

  • Zelensky said US negotiators raised the idea of Ukraine withdrawing from the Donbas in exchange for NATO membership or NATO-like security guarantees, and said it was the first time this linkage was raised in talks.
  • Zelensky quoted the US framing as: 'What if Ukraine joins NATO? Would Ukraine withdraw from Donbas?'
  • US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff met Putin in Moscow and then met Zelensky in Dublin, Ireland, on Thursday.
  • Zelensky said he would put a proposal to a national referendum if it included security guarantees, no constraints on Ukraine's military, and funds to rebuild the country.
  • Zelensky reiterated that Ukraine is not ready to concede sovereign territory to Russia.
  • Zelensky said that Putin's signals make this an 'extremely difficult conversation' and that 'NATO is back on the agenda.'
  • Zelensky dismissed Putin's proposed humanitarian ceasefire in Kostiantynivka as a ploy to allow Russian forces to occupy the city, saying civilians had already had opportunity to evacuate.
  • Zelensky said he opposed starting with a broad European security framework before ending the war, stating the war must be ended first and that changing the continent's security architecture first cannot happen.
  • Russia has revived 2021-era demands regarding NATO's configuration/expansion in eastern Europe, which Zelensky characterized as concerning.
  • The comments were made Friday but released by Zelensky's office on Saturday.
  • Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022 and currently occupies roughly a fifth of Ukrainian territory, including most of Donbas (Donetsk and Luhansk).
