Consensus Facts

  • Maria Bartiromo is out at Fox News Media, effective September 3, 2026, after 12½ years with the company.
  • Fox issued a brief statement: 'We thank Maria for her work over the last 12 ½ years and wish her all the best on her next chapter,' giving no official specific reason in the announcement itself.
  • The final episode of 'Mornings with Maria' aired Thursday morning on Fox Business Network; Bartiromo did not appear for an on-air farewell.
  • Bartiromo had not appeared on air since August 9, 2026, roughly a month before the announcement.
  • 'Mornings with Maria' will be renamed 'Mornings with FOX Business' with rotating anchors; 'Maria Bartiromo's Wall Street' will become 'FBN's Wall Street' (Cheryl Casone hosting this week per CNN, Variety, THR), and 'Sunday Morning Futures' will be hosted by Jason Chaffetz, a former Republican congressman, until a permanent host is named.
  • Bartiromo joined Fox in 2013/2014 after roughly two decades at CNBC, where she was known for pioneering live reporting from the New York Stock Exchange floor.
  • Bartiromo was among the Fox anchors named in the Dominion Voting Systems $1.6 billion defamation suit over 2020 election falsehoods, which Fox settled for $787.5 million in April 2023.
  • In June 2026, Bartiromo read an on-air apology tied to comments Kevin O'Leary made on her show claiming China funded opponents of his Utah data center; Fox and O'Leary now face a defamation suit over those comments.
  • Bartiromo is closely associated with President Trump, who has praised her repeatedly and appointed her to the Kennedy Center board in 2025.
  • Her departure is repeatedly compared to Fox's earlier ouster of Tucker Carlson in 2023.
