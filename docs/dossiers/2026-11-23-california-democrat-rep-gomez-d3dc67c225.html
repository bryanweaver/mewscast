Consensus Facts

  • U.S. Rep. Jimmy Gomez, a California Democrat, announced Monday he will resign from Congress effective Dec. 1, 2026.
  • Gomez is 51 years old and was a five-term, married congressman representing part of Los Angeles (California's 34th District).
  • The House Ethics Committee released a report on Monday finding substantial/credible evidence that Gomez engaged in sexual misconduct toward a female House staffer.
  • The committee found Gomez made unwelcome sexual advances and sexual contact and disregarded the power imbalance between House members and staff.
  • The report stated Gomez's behavior could constitute criminal misdemeanor sexual abuse; no criminal charges have been filed.
  • In a statement, Gomez said he had strong disagreements with the committee's findings but saw no alternative than to resign, and said he takes full responsibility for personal mistakes committed outside his marriage.
  • The staffer (referred to as 'Witness 1') alleged two incidents: one at a Washington bar in February 2024 where Gomez put his hand down her skirt, and one in his congressional office where she described him as 'forceful.'
  • The incidents originated around a birthday party hosted by another lawmaker; the woman described the bar incident as nonconsensual.
  • The report said Gomez's conduct caused the staffer significant and ongoing distress and contributed to her decision to leave House employment.
  • The committee noted it did not have enough time to pursue an adequate House sanction because Gomez lost his reelection bid, but published the report so the House could decide whether to sanction him.
  • Gomez lost his reelection this month to fellow Democrat Angela Gonzales-Torres by roughly 25 percentage points.
  • The staffer is represented by attorneys Lisa J. Banks and Sarah/Sara E. Nesbitt of Katz Banks Kumin, who applauded the report and accused Gomez of abusing his power.
  • The committee formally announced its investigation into Gomez in August 2026.
  • The investigation traces back to a report that Gomez was seen kissing a younger staffer at a party hosted by then-Congressman Eric Swalwell, who had resigned over his own sexual misconduct allegations.
