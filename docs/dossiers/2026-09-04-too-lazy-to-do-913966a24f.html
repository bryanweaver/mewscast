Consensus Facts

  • The Wall Street Journal published a report (dated Sept. 3-4, 2026) alleging that Pennsylvania Democratic Sen. John Fetterman shows little interest in the day-to-day work of being a senator, routinely skipping meetings with constituents and hearings.
  • The WSJ report described an incident in which Fetterman canceled on a group of paralyzed Pennsylvania military veterans, telling staff he was unwell, but then came into the office shortly after to do a Fox News interview.
  • The WSJ reported text messages in which Fetterman questioned why he had to keep meeting with the Children's Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP), and after an aide cited concerns about vaccines and Medicaid cuts, replied 'Well I'm not' and said he was 'convinced they just want free trips to DC.'
  • The WSJ reported that Fetterman balked at attending a funeral for three police officers killed near York, Pa., calling the three-hour ceremony a 'nonstarter,' and ultimately skipped it.
  • Rep. Brendan Boyle (D-Pa.) wrote on X that 'John Fetterman is a disgrace' and called him 'a lazy trust fund baby who is too lazy to do the job.'
  • Rep. Chris Deluzio (D-Pa.) wrote on X that Fetterman is 'not up for the high office he was elected to' and that Pennsylvania Democrats 'will do something about it in 2028,' citing 'laziness, disrespect, and willingness to carry water for a foreign government.'
  • Fetterman, 57, elected in 2022, has drifted rightward on issues including Israel, has become a top Senate supporter of Israel, and has faced questions about possibly leaving the Democratic Party.
  • Fetterman's office did not immediately respond to requests for comment following the report.
  • Fetterman's term ends in 2028.
