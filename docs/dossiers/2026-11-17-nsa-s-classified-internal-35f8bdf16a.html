Consensus Facts

  • The NSA's internal classified communications network, known as NSANet (or 'NSA Net'), went down on Tuesday, November 17, 2026.
  • NSANet is used by NSA employees to share and store classified information internally.
  • In an official statement, the NSA said it 'experienced temporary degradation during scheduled routine maintenance' and that 'mission-critical capabilities remain fully operational.'
  • U.S. officials said there is no evidence the outage was caused by a cyberattack.
  • The NSA staggered arrival times for non-emergency personnel at its Fort Meade, Maryland, headquarters in response to the outage.
  • The outage occurred during maintenance/system patching of the network, according to sources.
  • CNN first reported the story.
  • The NSA outage followed a series of recent unrelated outages affecting the FAA (nationwide ground stop), Microsoft (Outlook and Teams), and banks/Chase.
  • President Donald Trump, traveling in Asia (the Philippines), downplayed the outages, saying words to the effect of 'things like that happen' and 'it's fine.'
  • The NSA's infrastructure is described as having multiple layers of redundancy.
