Consensus Facts

  • President Trump announced on Friday, Sept. 18, 2026, an agreement with Denmark and Greenland that he said gives the US 'permanent control over security, and all other needs' in Greenland.
  • Trump made the announcement in a post on Truth Social.
  • The deal was described by Trump as stopping short of an outright US takeover or acquisition of Greenland, which had been his prior stated goal.
  • Trump said the US will immediately begin developing a 'large Military presence in the appropriate part of Greenland, of which there are many.'
  • Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen said the agreement recognizes the sovereignty and territorial integrity of the Kingdom and the right of the Greenlandic people to self-determination.
  • Frederiksen said the deal strengthens common security in the Arctic and North Atlantic area and is good for NATO and Europe.
  • Greenland government leader Jens-Frederik Nielsen welcomed the deal, saying it recognizes Greenland's interests and is for the benefit of all.
  • The three governments (US, Denmark, Greenland) are expected to sign the agreement next week in connection with the UN General Assembly in New York.
  • The full official text of the deal was not immediately available at the time of reporting.
  • The deal bars US adversaries from establishing military bases or troop presence in Greenland and restricts sensitive investments (including from Russia and China) without US written approval.
  • Greenland has hosted a US military presence for decades under an existing defense arrangement dating to the 1950s.
  • Trump had escalated efforts to acquire Greenland earlier in 2026, including threats and refusal to rule out force, prompting opposition from Denmark, Greenland, and other NATO members.
