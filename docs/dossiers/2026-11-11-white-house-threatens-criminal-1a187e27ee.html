Consensus Facts

  • White House counsel Will Scharf sent a letter dated Monday to Rep. Robert Garcia, D-Calif., the incoming chairman of the House Oversight Committee (NBC News, CNN, CBS News).
  • Scharf warned that Democrats' planned investigations of the Trump administration 'may cross the line into blatant criminality' and could warrant executive-branch probes (NBC News, CBS News).
  • Scharf cited the constitutional requirement that congressional investigations be tied to a 'legitimate legislative purpose' and accused Democrats of 'abusive and capricious threats' (NBC News, CBS News).
  • Garcia responded that 'Congress' authority comes from Article I of the Constitution, not from the permission of the White House,' said Congress has 'wide latitude to conduct investigations and oversight,' and said 'It appears Donald Trump has something to hide' (NBC News, CNN, CBS News).
  • Garcia said Democrats 'will not be deterred by baseless threats from a weaponized DOJ or from the President's lawyers' (NBC News, CBS News).
  • House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries, poised to become speaker in January, criticized the letter, saying a Democratic Congress would be 'a check and balance on an out-of-control executive branch' and telling the White House to 'consult the Constitution' (NBC News, CBS News).
  • Garcia and incoming Democratic chairs are set to take control of committees in January and have previewed probes into Trump, his family, businesses, and administration (NBC News, CNN, CBS News).
