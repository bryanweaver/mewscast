Consensus Facts

  • President Trump announced Thursday that an agreement has been reached for Hamas to disarm and for Israel to withdraw its forces from Gaza (AP, CBS, BBC, NPR, ABC).
  • Trump made the announcement via a Truth Social / social media post, framing it as part of the 20-point peace plan he offered previously (CBS, ABC; AP quotes the same post).
  • Trump stated the agreement would be carried out in 'carefully structured phases,' with Israeli forces withdrawing as disarmament is completed (AP, CBS, ABC).
  • Under the plan, an International Stabilization Force and a new Palestinian police force would take responsibility for security in Gaza (AP, CBS, ABC).
  • The announcement came roughly nine months after a U.S.-brokered Gaza ceasefire was signed (AP, CBS, NPR).
  • As of the announcement, neither Israel nor Hamas had issued a formal public statement acknowledging the deal, though a senior Hamas official communicated agreement through mediators/BBC (CBS, BBC, ABC, NPR).
  • A Hamas official said Hamas would only give up its heavy weapons if Israel withdraws from Gaza first (BBC, NPR; corroborated by Reuters headline).
  • The negotiations stem from a 20-point peace proposal Trump offered to end the Israel-Hamas war (CBS, ABC).
  • Mediators from Egypt, Qatar and Turkey were credited/thanked for their work on the plan (NPR, ABC).
  • The 'Board of Peace,' a Trump-backed multinational body, published a roadmap and stated Hamas agreed to it (CBS, BBC, ABC).
