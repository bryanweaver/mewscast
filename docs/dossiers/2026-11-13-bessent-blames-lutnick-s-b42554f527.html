Consensus Facts

  • Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told CNBC that Treasury and other agencies had favored putting Singapore-based Megaspeed on the Commerce Department's Entity List for months, but 'Commerce has the pen' and directed questions to Commerce's internal processes.
  • Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has agreed to testify before the Senate Banking Committee next month over the Commerce Department's role in the export of advanced AI chips (CBS cites Dec. 10; CNBC confirms voluntary agreement).
  • Megaspeed is a Singapore-based company suspected of diverting/funneling Nvidia's advanced AI chips to China in circumvention of US export rules, and had not been added to the Entity List.
  • Jensen Huang resigned/was ousted as Nvidia CEO after admitting an affair with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive/employee whom US intelligence reportedly suspected of being a Chinese spy.
  • Bessent said he was 'frustrated' by the delays, noting President Trump made clear China should not receive Nvidia chips above the H100 level and that any 'leakage' hurts US companies in the AI race.
  • Bessent also pointed criticism at Nvidia, saying the 'real story' is whether Nvidia knew its client and made a robust effort to prevent sales to what were 'obviously pass-through shell companies.'
  • A Situation Room confrontation last week in which Bessent challenged Lutnick over his department's reluctance to act on Megaspeed was first reported by Axios and corroborated by CNN's sources.
  • Senate Banking Committee Chairman Sen. Tim Scott (R-S.C.) said Huang and Lutnick were asked to testify, and Republicans have raised national security concerns over the chip exports.

CNN: Two people familiar with the matter said Bessent confronted Lutnick last Thursday in the Situation Room; Lutnick did not immediately commit to any action.

Axios: Headline/report asserts the confrontation occurred and that Lutnick 'repeatedly stood in the way' of a crackdown (per CNBC's summary of Axios reporting).

CNBC: Lists 'Treasury, Defense and State and the Office of the U.S. Trade Representative.'
BBC News: Says several agencies 'were in favour' without a full enumeration.
Testimony date for Lutnick
CBS News: Specifies Lutnick agreed to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on Dec. 10, per a U.S. official.
CNBC: Says Lutnick 'proactively agreed to appear voluntarily' next month per Commerce spokeswoman, without specifying Dec. 10.
BBC News: Says Lutnick was summoned to appear before the committee in December, no specific date.
Scale and nature of Megaspeed
CNN: Originally incorporated in 2015 as Chinese video game developer '7Road International'; renamed after Alice Huang became director; now one of Asia's largest Nvidia buyers, purchasing more than $4 billion in semiconductors by some analyst estimates.
