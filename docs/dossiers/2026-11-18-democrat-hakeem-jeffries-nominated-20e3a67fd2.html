Consensus Facts

  • House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries to be speaker of the House in a closed-door vote on Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026 (AP, CNN).
  • The nomination vote was 251-11, with 11 Democrats voting against Jeffries on a secret ballot (AP, CNN).
  • If elected by the full House, Jeffries would become the first Black speaker of the House in US history (AP, CNN; echoed across all outlet headlines).
  • A bloc of seven newly elected progressive/Democratic Socialist members voted 'no,' citing unmet policy demands including votes on Medicare for All and ending the war in Gaza (AP, CNN).
  • The seven signatories to the dissent statement were Reps.-elect Darializa Avila Chevalier (NY), Adam Hamawy (NJ), Frederick Haynes (TX), Melat Kiros (CO), Donavan McKinney (MI), Chris Rabb (PA), and Claire Valdez (NY) (AP, CNN).
  • Jeffries responded to dissenters by saying, 'Our job is to fight MAGA extremism, not each other' (AP, CNN).
  • Jeffries' leadership team includes Rep. Katherine Clark, set to be the first female House majority leader, and Rep. Pete Aguilar, set to be the first Latino majority whip (AP, CNN).
  • Rep. Jim Clyburn, a senior Democrat, delivered a powerful endorsement of Jeffries inside the room during the nomination (AP, CNN).
  • Jeffries will need a simple majority (218 votes) in a full House floor vote when the new Congress convenes on Jan. 3, 2027 (AP, CNN).
  • The nomination follows Democratic midterm gains that gave the party a large incoming House majority (AP, CNN).
