Consensus Facts

  • Catastrophic flash floods struck the Nepal-China border region on Wednesday, Aug. 26, 2026.
  • The floods were triggered by a glacier collapse in the Himalayas that also formed a new upstream lake, prompting warnings of further flooding.
  • By Saturday, Aug. 29, the death toll in Nepal exceeded 670 (AP: 669; CNN: 675), with roughly 2,400-2,500 people still missing in Nepal.
  • Chinese authorities reported at least seven deaths in Tibet, with more than 550 missing, and official reports out of Tibet have been slow.
  • Roughly 3,000 people remained missing across Nepal and China combined as of Saturday.
  • More than 3,700 people have been rescued in Nepal, including aerial evacuations by army helicopter.
  • Nepal's Nuwakot district — including the village of Trishuli/Devighat — was among the hardest hit, with homes buried in mud and debris and residents returning to salvage belongings.
  • Excavation equipment and heavy machinery were brought into flooded villages to dig through rubble.
  • Hundreds of the missing are foreigners who were in the area to work, trek, or make a pilgrimage to a sacred Hindu site (Mount Kailash).
  • Rescuers reached a devastated Chinese border-crossing area (Gyirong Port) and described finding little but ruins/debris, with no sign of survivors.
