Consensus Facts

  • Residents of the Russian-occupied city of Oleshky in Ukraine's Kherson region were evacuated and crossed into Ukrainian-controlled territory on Saturday, October 31, 2026.
  • The evacuation took place under a localized humanitarian ceasefire in the Kherson region that began at 3am local time on Tuesday and is expected to continue through Sunday evening, November 1.
  • US First Lady Melania Trump was credited with helping broker the ceasefire that enabled the evacuation.
  • Evacuees chose between traveling to Ukrainian- or Russian-held territory; those heading to Ukraine traveled through Russia and then flew to Homel (Gomel), Belarus, before crossing into Ukraine.
  • Melania Trump posted on social media Friday that the humanitarian convoy could safely evacuate more than 750 people.
  • The ceasefire is described as the longest/most significant/widespread truce since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022.
  • Several hundred residents chose to remain in Oleshky despite devastation and the lack of gas, electricity, and running water.
  • Russia and Ukraine did not specify exactly how many people chose to leave the city.
  • Dmytro Lubinets, Ukraine's human rights commissioner/ombudsman, accompanied evacuees and became the first Ukrainian official since the start of the war to visit Russian-occupied parts of Ukraine.
  • Russia said it had prepared 'dozens of tonnes' of aid (food, medicine, hygiene products, generator fuel) for residents who stayed.
  • Fighting continued elsewhere in Ukraine during the localized ceasefire, including Russian strikes on Ukrainian energy and power infrastructure.
  • Emotional family reunions occurred at the border, including relatives who had not seen each other for over two years.
