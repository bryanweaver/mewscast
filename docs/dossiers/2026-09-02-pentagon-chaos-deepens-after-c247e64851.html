  • Driscoll's resignation followed months of tension and conflict with Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth.
  • Driscoll spoke with President Trump when he submitted his resignation and raised concerns about the state and direction of the Army.
  • Driscoll is a close ally and friend of Vice President JD Vance, whom he met as a classmate at Yale Law School.
  • Driscoll became known as Trump's 'drone guy' for his focus on military modernization and emerging battlefield technologies.
  • Hegseth has fired or sidelined more than a dozen senior military officers during his tenure at the Pentagon.
  • Former Army Chief of Staff Gen. Randy George was among those pushed out by Hegseth; George was a friend and ally of Driscoll.
  • Driscoll's resignation was first reported by The Wall Street Journal.
  • Navy Secretary John Phelan departed the Pentagon in April.
