Consensus Facts

  • Both NPR articles report that more than 30 homes have collapsed into the ocean in Dare County, North Carolina, since 2020.
  • Both articles report that roughly 100 additional homes in coastal North Carolina are threatened by erosion exacerbated by sea level rise.
  • Both articles attribute the collapses to FEMA's 'perverse incentives': FEMA flood insurance pays homeowners after a house collapses but not before, so owners have no financial choice but to let homes fall.
  • Both articles state the problem extends beyond North Carolina to other states, naming California, Michigan, and Maine among affected areas.
  • Both articles report that collapsing homes create debris and pollution that require costly taxpayer-funded cleanup and threaten beaches and wildlife.
  • Both articles are bylined by Rebecca Hersher and Ryan Kellman and frame the status quo as unsustainable while noting growing alarm among homeowners, local officials, and members of Congress.
