Consensus Facts

  • President Trump announced on Friday, November 6, 2026, via a Truth Social post that Stephen Miller will replace Susie Wiles as White House chief of staff.
  • Miller, who was serving as White House deputy chief of staff, will take over the chief of staff role starting Monday, November 9, 2026.
  • Trump described Miller as one of his most trusted advisors, and multiple outlets identify Miller as an architect of Trump's immigration enforcement and deportation policies.
  • Wiles ran Trump's 2024 presidential campaign and had served as his chief of staff since the start of his second administration.
  • Trump said Wiles will be moving over to the Trump Political Operation, and thanked her for her service.
  • The shakeup follows this week's midterm elections in which Democrats gained ground / Republicans lost power.
  • Wiles posted on X that Friday was her last day as chief of staff, calling the role the honor of a lifetime and saying her commitment to the president and mission would not change.

ABC News: Describes Miller neutrally as Trump's 'top aide.'
