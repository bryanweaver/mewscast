Consensus Facts

  • Romanian prosecutors (DIICOT, the anti-organized crime agency) formally indicted Andrew Tate on Friday, September 4, 2026, on charges including trafficking minors, sex with a minor, and money laundering.
  • Andrew Tate is 39; his brother Tristan Tate is 38, and Tristan was indicted on a complicity charge in the same case.
  • Both brothers hold dual U.S. and British citizenship and were first arrested in Bucharest, Romania, in late 2022.
  • DIICOT alleges the brothers recruited a 15-year-old girl in Luton, England, in 2012 and later repeatedly transported her to Romania.
  • Prosecutors allege the brothers obtained more than $1.2 million (roughly $1.25 million per one outlet) from the girl's webcam activity through coercion.
  • Tristan Tate is alleged to have known the victim's age and to have helped deceive her about Andrew's intentions across a period spanning roughly 2014 to 2021.
  • Prosecutors allege Andrew Tate repeatedly had sexual intercourse and sexual acts with a second 15-year-old girl between December 2020 and mid-2021.
  • Between December 2023 and July 2024, DIICOT alleges the brothers purchased four luxury vehicles worth about 825,000 euros ($957,000) to conceal money derived from human trafficking, and prosecutors requested the vehicles be confiscated.
  • Prosecutors allege victims were paid or intimidated in attempts to influence them not to give statements or to give false statements (witness tampering / attempting to influence statements).
  • An earlier 2023 indictment was returned to DIICOT by a Romanian court in 2024 due to multiple legal and procedural irregularities; the case was not closed, and a separate legal case also ran in Romania that year.
  • Both Tate brothers have denied all allegations, and Andrew Tate has characterized the prosecutions as a political effort to silence him.
  • The brothers are currently detained in Miami after the United Kingdom requested their extradition in July on separate sex crimes allegations, and they are contesting that extradition.
  • The brothers took the witness stand in Miami federal court in the week prior, arguing they cannot adequately mount a defense while jailed during extradition proceedings, and their lawyers argue the U.K. request is premature because the Romanian case takes precedence.
  • Much of the reporting is sourced to the Associated Press.
