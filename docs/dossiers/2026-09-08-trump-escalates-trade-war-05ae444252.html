Consensus Facts

  • On Sept. 8, 2026 (Tuesday), the Trump administration moved to block imports of certain Canadian goods, with the measures set to take effect in about three weeks — Sept. 29 (NBC News, AP).
  • The move came in direct response to Canadian retaliatory tariffs on about $20 billion worth of U.S. goods that took effect Tuesday (NBC News, AP, CNBC).
  • Trump threatened to ban the sale of Bombardier aircraft in the U.S. unless the company manufactures planes in the United States, posting the demand on Truth Social on Monday (AP, NBC News).
  • Bombardier responded that its supply chain relies heavily on U.S. companies and supports thousands of U.S. jobs (AP, NBC News).
  • Canada-U.S. trade talks collapsed on Aug. 21, with Prime Minister Mark Carney citing 'uneconomic'/'unfair' U.S. demands ('asked too much and offered too little'); the Trump administration disputed Carney's characterization (NBC News, CNBC, AP).
  • Trump posted a map on Truth Social on Monday depicting the U.S. flag covering North America, Greenland and Iceland, labeled 'United States of America' (BBC, AP, CNBC).
  • Iceland's Foreign Minister Þorgerður Katrín Gunnarsdóttir summoned U.S. Ambassador Billy Long over the map post, calling it inappropriate/unacceptable (BBC, AP).
  • Trump has repeatedly floated making Canada the 51st U.S. state and annexing/acquiring Greenland (BBC, AP, CNBC).
  • Trump renamed Lake Ontario as 'Lake America,' which Canadian officials said they would ignore (NBC News, AP).
