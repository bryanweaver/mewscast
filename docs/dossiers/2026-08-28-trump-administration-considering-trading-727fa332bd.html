Consensus Facts

  • The Trump administration is considering a land exchange that would give a private developer a parcel of Yosemite National Park land to build a road.
  • The developer is Kingsbarn Realty Capital, a Nevada-based real estate/investment firm founded and led by CEO Jeff Pori.
  • The road would connect Kingsbarn's adjacent private property to a Yosemite route, giving a planned development more direct access to the park.
  • Under the proposed arrangement, the Park Service would give up land or an interest/easement and in return receive land of equal value elsewhere in California that has not yet been determined.
  • The original reporting came from the Washington-based outlet NOTUS, published Friday, citing anonymous sources and agency documents.
  • The Department of the Interior acknowledged a proposal is under consideration but denied inappropriate influence and stated no final decisions have been made, emphasizing any deal would comply with federal laws and review processes.
  • Congressional Democrats, including Sen. Adam Schiff, have objected to the deal, with Schiff saying the administration 'appears hellbent on moving forward' and cares only about 'whether there is money involved.'
  • The Interior Department notified Congress earlier this year that it was considering enabling the exchange via the Land and Water Conservation Fund, drawing Democratic criticism that the fund is meant for conservation.
  • Conservation groups and park advocates have criticized the proposed deal.
