Consensus Facts

  • On Sunday, Aug. 16, 2026, President Donald Trump announced via Truth Social that he directed Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to 'substantially reduce' joint US-South Korea military exercises.
  • Trump cited his 'very good relationship' with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un as a reason, calling the drills a signal that is 'totally inappropriate and hostile.'
  • Trump complained the exercises are costly and largely paid for by the United States.
  • Trump noted that he had asked the President of South Korea to join the US in the 'Denuclearization' of Iran and was told 'No thanks!'
  • The exercises in question are the annual Ulchi Freedom Shield drills, scheduled to begin Monday, involving about 18,000 South Korean soldiers.
  • Trump said it was 'too late to cancel' the drills.
  • Earlier in the weekend, Trump posted an old 2018 photograph of himself and Kim, describing them as friends who get along 'GREAT.'
  • North Korea recently conducted a ballistic missile test that experts and officials assessed as a protest against the drills.
  • Trump has previously scaled back US-South Korea exercises, including a suspension in 2018 during denuclearization negotiations with Kim.
  • North Korea condemned the drills as a rehearsal for aggressive war.
