Suggested post type: REPORT — Only two outlets provided full body text and they align on the core facts, but the seven-outlet dossier shows materially different framings — procedural (CBS), partisan-conflict (CNN), solution-focused (Bloomberg), and adjacent-scandal angles (Politico, Washington Times) — making the coverage divergence itself the story worth reporting.

Consensus Facts

  • Republican Sen. John Cornyn of Texas blocked President Trump's nomination of T. Elliot Gaiser to a federal district court seat in Texas (CNN, CBS News).
  • Cornyn did so by withholding/declining to return the 'blue slip' for the Judiciary Committee, a Senate custom allowing home-state senators to block judgeships in their states (CNN, CBS News).
  • Gaiser currently serves as head of the Justice Department's Office of Legal Counsel (CNN, CBS News).
  • Cornyn objected because Gaiser is not a Texan, lacks trial experience, and was not properly vetted, saying the White House tried to 'jam' the Senate (CNN, CBS News).
  • Cornyn has offered the White House an alternative candidate for the seat (CNN, CBS News).
  • Gaiser did not appear at Wednesday's Judiciary Committee hearing, where seven other Trump judicial nominees testified (CNN, CBS News).
  • Gaiser authored a controversial legal opinion justifying U.S. military strikes on alleged drug-smuggling boats in the Caribbean/eastern Pacific (CNN, CBS News).
  • The nomination came during a lame-duck session as Republicans rush to confirm judges before Democrats take control of the Senate in January (CNN, CBS News).
  • Democrats are poised to control the Senate following the Nov. 3 midterm elections, making confirmation of Trump's remaining picks unlikely (CNN, CBS News).
