Consensus Facts

  • A 16-year-old gunman killed three children and a 25-year-old teacher at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday, Oct. 16, 2026.
  • The shooter was a former student who had attended special needs classes at the school, according to police.
  • The attacker shot his way through locked doors to enter the building; all victims were shot in one special needs classroom.
  • A teacher threw her phone at the gunman, then tackled and fought him on the floor, and police credited her with helping prevent further bloodshed; she was not injured.
  • A father of two students at the school, driving past as the attack unfolded, retrieved a pistol, followed the shooter inside, and fatally shot him.
  • The teacher was uninjured, while a 7-year-old child and a 54-year-old teacher were injured and hospitalized.
  • Police identified the youngest victim killed as 5-year-old Rhoam Summer and the slain teacher as Megan (Meghan) Lambert, 25; the two other children killed were 8 and 11.
  • The shooter's motive remains under investigation, and a search warrant was executed at his home.
  • The gunman had roughly 44 rounds of shotgun ammunition remaining when he was stopped, after firing as many as 15 shots.
  • Bend Police Chief Mike Krantz praised the teacher's and the father's actions as heroic and lifesaving.
  • President Donald Trump publicly praised the armed bystander as a hero who prevented the situation from becoming worse.
