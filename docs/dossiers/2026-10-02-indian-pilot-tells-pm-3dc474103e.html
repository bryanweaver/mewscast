Consensus Facts

  • Flydubai captain Smit Machchhar, an Indian national, was attacked by his co-pilot during flight FZ1073 bound for Tel Aviv from Dubai on Wednesday (reported by CBS News, BBC News, NBC News, CNN).
  • Despite being stabbed and lying injured on the cockpit floor, Machchhar managed to open the cockpit door so passengers could enter and subdue the attacking co-pilot (CBS News, BBC News, NBC News).
  • Machchhar described the moment in an emotional video call with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi from his hospital bed (CBS News, BBC News, CNN).
  • Machchhar said 'I was fighting for my life' but was determined not to let others die (CBS News, BBC News, CNN).
  • There were 174 people on board the aircraft — Machchhar plus 173 others (CBS News, BBC News states more than 170 passengers).
  • After the attacker was overpowered, reserve/other pilots on board landed the plane safely in Saudi Arabia (CBS News references passengers subdued the attacker; BBC News specifies two reserve pilots landed the plane at Tabuk Airport).
  • Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu stated investigations indicated the attacker had undergone 'Islamist radical indoctrination' and that the attacker was suicidal, shouting 'kill me, kill me' after being subdued (CBS News, BBC News).
  • Machchhar was taken to hospital in Saudi Arabia and is reported to be in stable condition / recovering well (CBS News, BBC News).
