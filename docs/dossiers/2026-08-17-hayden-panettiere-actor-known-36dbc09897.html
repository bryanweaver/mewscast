Consensus Facts

  • Actress Hayden Panettiere died at the age of 36.
  • Her death was announced by her father, Skip Panettiere, in a statement provided through her representative/publicist.
  • Skip Panettiere's statement described her as 'an incredible light and a force of nature' and asked for privacy 'as our family takes time to process this unimaginable loss.'
  • She died Sunday (Aug. 16, 2026) in Greenville, South Carolina.
  • Police responded to a report of an unresponsive person; an acquaintance of Panettiere placed the 911 call.
  • The Greenville County Coroner's Office said she was found in cardiac arrest and that advanced cardiac life support measures were initiated but resuscitation efforts were unsuccessful.
  • An autopsy completed Monday found no signs of trauma that would have contributed to the death.
  • The cause and manner of death remain pending further investigation and additional studies.
  • Police said the preliminary investigation indicated no signs of foul play or suspicious circumstances.
  • She rose to fame as cheerleader Claire Bennet in the series 'Heroes' (2006-2010).
  • She played country singer Juliette Barnes in the TV drama 'Nashville,' a role that earned her two Golden Globe nominations.
  • She began acting as a very young child, appearing on soap operas including 'One Life to Live,' and voiced Dot in Pixar's 'A Bug's Life.'
  • Her film credits include 'Remember the Titans' and the 'Scream' franchise.
  • In May 2026 she published a memoir titled 'This Is Me: A Reckoning,' addressing her struggles with addiction and depression.
  • She experienced postpartum depression after the birth of her daughter, Kaya, with former partner Wladimir Klitschko, and gave up custody in 2018.
  • Her younger brother, actor Jansen Panettiere, died of a heart condition/complications (reported as 2023 by CBS/NBC and 2025 by LA Times).
  • SAG-AFTRA and actress Viola Davis, who co-starred with Panettiere in the 2016 film 'Custody,' paid public tribute.
