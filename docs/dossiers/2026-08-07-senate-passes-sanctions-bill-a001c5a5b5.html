Consensus Facts

  • The Senate passed a sanctions bill targeting Russia and Iran on Friday, Aug. 7, 2026.
  • The vote was 86-11 (reported by NBC News, Axios, CBS News, and ABC News).
  • The bill was renamed in honor of the late Republican Sen. Lindsey Graham of South Carolina, its lead proponent, who died suddenly in July 2026.
  • The full title is The Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 (NBC News and CBS News; Axios uses a shorter variant omitting 'Iran').
  • The bill allows the president to impose tariffs of up to 100% on the largest buyers of Russian oil and gas, limited to the top five importers (NBC News, Axios, CBS News).
  • The legislation includes additional sanctions on Russian President Vladimir Putin, government officials, oligarchs, and Russian financial institutions (NBC News, CBS News).
  • The bill extends or renews sanctions on Iran's energy and weapons sectors, added at the urging of President Trump (NBC News, Axios, CBS News).
  • Sen. Rand Paul, R-Ky., secured a vote on an amendment to strip the tariff authority from the bill, which failed (Axios, CBS News).
  • The bill passed with overwhelming bipartisan support and now heads to the Republican-controlled House, where its future is uncertain (NBC News, CBS News, ABC News).
  • Graham championed the bill alongside Sen. Richard Blumenthal, D-Conn. (NBC News, Axios, CBS News).
