Consensus Facts

  • Republican Rep. Thomas Massie (R-Ky.) introduced/reintroduced articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth on the House floor on Monday, Nov. 9, 2026.
  • The central charge is that Hegseth waged the Iran war without a declaration of war or congressional authorization, in violation of the Constitution and the War Powers Resolution.
  • Because Massie filed the measure as a privileged resolution, House Republican leaders must bring it up within two legislative days, forcing a floor vote.
  • Massie is an outgoing/retiring congressman and a longtime Trump critic who lost his primary after Trump endorsed his opponent.
  • A simple majority is required for the articles to pass the House; if passed, the matter would go to the Senate, where a two-thirds majority would be required to convict and remove Hegseth.
  • Hegseth announced he would hold a press conference at the Pentagon on Tuesday morning to address the impeachment effort, and said he is 'not worried' about it.
  • The effort faces long odds, with the House still under Republican control and the Senate threshold considered unlikely to be met.
