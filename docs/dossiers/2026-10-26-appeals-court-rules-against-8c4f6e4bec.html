Consensus Facts

  • A three-judge panel of the 9th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals ruled 2-1 against the Trump Justice Department's emergency request to force California to set aside roughly 20,000 mail-in ballots from people the administration claims are likely noncitizens.
  • The majority held the appeals court lacked jurisdiction at this stage because the district court had not yet been given adequate opportunity to rule, sending the case back to the U.S. District Court in California.
  • The panel consisted of Judge Mark Bennett (a Trump appointee) and Judge Gabriel Sanchez (a Biden appointee) in the majority, with Judge Patrick Bumatay (a Trump appointee) dissenting.
  • The majority wrote that 'The United States's claimed emergency, at least in part, is a self-constructed one,' attributing the delay in part to DOJ's own late filing.
  • The DOJ filed its lawsuit against California earlier this month (Oct. 15), after Acting Deputy AG Trent McCotter gave officials 48 hours to review a list of suspected noncitizen voters.
  • DOJ took the unusual step of appealing to the 9th Circuit before the district court ruled, and the panel held a rare emergency Sunday oral argument (held via videoconference/Zoom).
  • Bennett repeatedly questioned whether the appeals court had jurisdiction and raised constitutional concerns rooted in the Elections Clause, which gives states (and Congress) primary authority over how elections are run.
  • DOJ attorney Matthew Donnelly argued the way California administers its elections infringes on the federal government's ability to enforce the federal statute barring noncitizen voting.
  • A district court hearing on DOJ's request for a temporary restraining order was scheduled for Tuesday afternoon.
  • The lawsuit was filed less than three weeks before the Nov. 3 midterm elections, while California was already processing hundreds of thousands of mail ballots.
  • California officials said the DOJ's list of 20,000 alleged noncitizens was unreliable; Judge Sanchez pressed Donnelly on the reliability and sourcing of the list, and Donnelly said the data came from a purchased list of California voters analyzed by third parties.

Disagreements

CBS News: Los Angeles County has already processed an estimated 550,000 mail ballots, according to court filings.
CNN: Los Angeles County has already processed more than 300,000 midterm ballots (and 'more than 300,000 mail ballots had already been processed').
ABC News: References mail-in ballots already being processed numbering 'in the hundreds of thousands' without a specific LA County figure.
Characterization of the ruling's scope
CBS News: Frames it as a 'dismissal' of DOJ's effort on jurisdictional grounds, noting Bennett would have rejected the merits too.
CNN: Frames it as a rejection of an 'unprecedented' attempt, emphasizing both procedural ruling and skepticism on the merits.
ABC News: Frames it as the court 'declining to step in' / 'punting it back to district court.'
NBC News: Earlier-stage piece (Reuters wire) framing the panel as merely 'appearing skeptical' during Sunday argument, before any ruling.
Whether and how many people were criminally charged from the list
ABC News (Article 6): Notes DOJ announced charges of 'roughly 28 individuals from the list accused of voting and immigration-related crimes.'
NBC News: Cites a Reuters investigation finding just 129 cases nationally in which noncitizens were federally charged with voting illegally, many stemming from confusion or error.
CBS News / CNN / ABC (Article 4): Do not mention the 28 charged figure in their ruling coverage.
Underlying motive attributed to DOJ
CNN (Article 7): States the action is one of many steps to 'try to help prove President Donald Trump's baseless claims of massive voter fraud,' and links it to a separate Alaska suit.
NBC News: Says the lawsuit 'signals the willingness of Trump's administration to contest midterm results.'
CBS News / ABC News: Report the dispute more descriptively, quoting Newsom and officials but not characterizing DOJ's claims as 'baseless' in their own voice.
