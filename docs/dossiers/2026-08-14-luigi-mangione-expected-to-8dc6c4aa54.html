Consensus Facts

  • Luigi Mangione, 28, is expected to plead guilty in Manhattan federal court on Friday in connection with the 2024 killing of UnitedHealthcare CEO Brian Thompson.
  • Mangione faces two federal charges: interstate stalking resulting in death and stalking through use of interstate facilities resulting in death, each carrying a maximum penalty of life in prison.
  • The expected plea is reported via sources familiar with the matter; prosecutors, defense attorneys, and court/DOJ officials declined to comment.
  • A pretrial hearing/conference is scheduled for 11 a.m. ET at Manhattan federal court.
  • U.S. District Judge Margaret Garnett blocked/threw out the death-penalty-eligible murder and firearm charges in January 2026, narrowing the federal case to two stalking counts.
  • Mangione originally pleaded not guilty in both the federal and separate state cases.
  • Mangione's state murder trial is scheduled to begin in early September 2026 (ABC News specifies Sept. 8).
  • In the state case, Mangione remains charged with second-degree murder and multiple weapons-related counts.
  • Mangione was arrested at a McDonald's in Pennsylvania (Altoona) following a multi-day manhunt after Thompson was shot outside a Manhattan hotel hosting an investor conference in December 2024.
  • Authorities recovered a notebook attributed to Mangione that prosecutors say detailed grievances against and/or plans to kill Thompson.
  • If Mangione pleads guilty federally, his defense is expected to seek dismissal of the state case on double jeopardy grounds, while state prosecutors argue the stalking and murder charges address different harms.
  • Nothing is final until Mangione himself accepts a deal, and he could reverse his decision even during the hearing.
