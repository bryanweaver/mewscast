Consensus Facts

  • The FAA issued a nationwide ground stop for all departing U.S. flights on Friday, Nov. 13, 2026, citing 'extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety.' (NBC News, CNN, CBS News, NPR, CNBC)
  • The ground stop was attributed to a temporary telecommunications/communications outage affecting air traffic control facilities. (NBC News, CNN, CBS News, NPR, CNBC)
  • Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said FAA technicians are investigating the issue and would provide immediate updates. (NBC News, CBS News, NPR, CNBC)
  • The communication issues affected multiple Air Route Traffic Control Centers, which guide aircraft at high altitudes. (CNBC, CBS News, NPR)
  • Inbound international flights would be managed on a case-by-case basis while departing traffic was halted until further notice. (NBC News, CNN, CBS News, NPR, CNBC)
  • Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there was no indication of a threat to the U.S. and nothing 'nefarious' behind the outage as of that point. (NBC News, CNN, CBS News, CNBC)
  • President Donald Trump was briefed on the situation. (NBC News, CNN)
  • The FAA said flights were halted out of an 'abundance of caution' and that every flight had been accounted for. (CNBC, NPR, CBS News)
  • Planes already in the air would continue their flights. (CNN, CNBC)
