Consensus Facts

  • Nidal Malik Hasan, a former US Army major and psychiatrist, was convicted of killing 13 people and injuring/wounding more than 30 others in a 2009 shooting at Fort Hood, Texas.
  • Hasan's execution is scheduled for December 3 at Fort Hood, Texas, at 1 p.m. Central Time.
  • Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said in an interview with Jack Posobiec on Real America's Voice that the execution would be made 'public,' saying 'We'll make sure that people are able to watch it, that it's public, because people need to understand that there's serious consequences for these types of things.'
  • A Pentagon/Defense Department official clarified that the execution would be livestreamed, with more details to follow.
  • President Donald Trump approved/ordered the execution and authorized the use of a firing squad.
  • Hasan will be executed by firing squad, a method the US military has not used since 1945 (the execution of Pvt. Eddie Slovik for desertion during World War II).
  • This would be the first US military execution in decades — reported as the first since 1961 (when a soldier was hanged).
  • No state or federal execution has been televised in the US, and the last public execution in the country took place in 1936 in Kentucky.
  • Hasan could still seek habeas corpus relief in federal court, which could delay the execution.
  • The firing squad decision came days after the botched lethal-injection execution of Christa Pike in Tennessee.
  • Hasan was sentenced to death following his 2013 court-martial, in which he represented himself.
  • The Supreme Court declined to review Hasan's case/conviction last year (2024 petition).
