Consensus Facts

  • A van drove into a crowd near Berlin's Christopher Street Day (LGBTQ+ pride) event on Saturday night, July 25, 2026, killing one person.
  • At least 15 to 16 people were injured, some with life-threatening injuries (NBC News reports at least 15; ABC News reports about 16).
  • According to police, a white van drove into Tiergarten Park — just off the route the pride march had passed along earlier — and hit several people before colliding with a tree.
  • Police called off the pride event and interrupted a band's stage performance near the Brandenburg Gate around 10:15 p.m. local time, urging people to leave the area immediately.
  • Police officer Florian Nath said in a video on the Berlin police X account that an intensive search for possible suspects was underway.
  • Hundreds of thousands of people had gathered in Berlin for the Christopher Street Day parade, one of Europe's largest LGBTQ+ celebrations, and had celebrated peacefully with around 80 floats before the incident.
