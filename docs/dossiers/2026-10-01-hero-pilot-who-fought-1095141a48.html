Consensus Facts

  • A Flydubai flight (FZ1073) from Dubai to Tel Aviv was diverted after one pilot stabbed the other in the cockpit (Al Jazeera, BBC, CNN).
  • Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said the attacker appeared to be trying to crash the plane (Al Jazeera, BBC, CNN).
  • The stabbed captain was identified as Indian national Capt. Smit Machchhar, hailed as a hero by Netanyahu (Al Jazeera, BBC).
  • An Israeli passenger (named Yaniv Hayun/Hayoun) entered the cockpit, helped subdue the attacker, and grabbed the plane's controls before a pilot took over (Al Jazeera, BBC, CNN).
  • The plane made an emergency landing at Tabuk airport in northwestern Saudi Arabia (Al Jazeera, BBC).
  • The attacking pilot was arrested and interrogated by Saudi authorities (Al Jazeera, BBC).
  • Netanyahu said the captain saved the lives of 174 people on board (Al Jazeera, BBC).
  • The aircraft experienced a rapid descent of over 17,000 feet in roughly one to two minutes according to Flightradar24 data (Al Jazeera, BBC).
  • Flydubai described the event as an 'altercation' in the cockpit and said on-duty crew diverted and landed the aircraft safely (Al Jazeera, BBC).
  • The Indian embassy reported Machchhar was hospitalized in Tabuk in stable condition (Al Jazeera, BBC).
  • No official motive for the attack had been established at the time of reporting (Al Jazeera, BBC).
