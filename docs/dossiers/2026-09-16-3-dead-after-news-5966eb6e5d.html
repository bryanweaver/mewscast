Consensus Facts

  • A news helicopter used by NBC4 Los Angeles and Telemundo 52 crashed in the Chatsworth neighborhood of the San Fernando Valley on Tuesday, Sept. 15, 2026, shortly before 7 p.m. local time.
  • Three people were killed: aerial photojournalist/reporter Eliana Moreno, pilot George Marciniw, and a third victim on the ground identified as 29-year-old Edy Gutierrez Mejia.
  • Moreno and Marciniw were employees of Angel City Air, the company that operated the helicopter for the station.
  • The helicopter was covering a nearby collision in which an SUV struck a Los Angeles Metro city bus, killing two people and injuring six others.
  • The aircraft involved was a Eurocopter AS350 (AS 350 B2).
  • The helicopter burst into flames on impact, and the fire spread to nearby vehicles and two storage containers.
  • Eliana Moreno was born in Orange County and earned a broadcast journalism and political science degree from Chapman University in 2010, the same year she joined Angel City Air.
  • The National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) is leading the investigation into the crash.
  • Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass and California Gov. Gavin Newsom publicly offered condolences over the bus and helicopter crashes.
  • The crash occurred while multiple news helicopters were in the area covering the bus collision.
