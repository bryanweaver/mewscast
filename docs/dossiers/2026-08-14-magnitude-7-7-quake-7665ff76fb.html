Consensus Facts

  • A magnitude-7.7 earthquake struck off the coast of Indonesia in the Flores region, according to the USGS.
  • The epicenter was located approximately 68 km (42 miles) north-northwest of the city of Ende.
  • The quake struck at 5:58 a.m. local time on Saturday (Aug. 15) Indonesia time.
  • Indonesian authorities (BMKG) issued a tsunami warning following the quake.
  • The initial quake was followed by aftershocks.
  • Buildings, including facilities in Maumere, were reported damaged.
  • Australia's tsunami warning center said the quake posed no tsunami threat to the Australian mainland, islands, or territories.
  • As of reporting, officials had not confirmed any casualties.
  • Indonesia is prone to earthquakes due to its location on the Pacific 'Ring of Fire.'
