Consensus Facts

  • Lindsay Clancy's murder trial continued Wednesday, Aug. 19, 2026, in Plymouth Superior Court in Massachusetts (Day 16).
  • Judge William Sullivan sent jurors home early Wednesday afternoon due to what he called an 'unforeseen circumstance,' telling them not to speculate about it or hold it against either side.
  • Sullivan said proceedings remain on schedule and court will resume at 9 a.m. Thursday.
  • Forensic/clinical psychologist Dr. Paul Zeizel completed his testimony for the defense on Wednesday.
  • Zeizel testified that Clancy had 'bipolar disorder with postpartum psychosis.'
  • Zeizel testified that Clancy said she heard a voice ordering her to kill her children.
  • Zeizel testified that Clancy 'really loved' her children, misses them, and thinks of them 'every single day, almost every moment of the day.'
  • The judge ruled that Emily Thorndike, a TikTok user and licensed clinical social worker who formerly worked at McLean Hospital, would not be allowed to testify for the defense.
  • Thorndike did not work at McLean while Clancy was treated there, and her potential testimony disputed how staffing/treatment at McLean was described during the trial.
  • The judge heard Thorndike's potential testimony (voir dire) outside the jury's presence before ruling.
  • Defense attorney Kevin Reddington is leading the defense, arguing Clancy suffered postpartum psychosis and should not be held criminally responsible.
  • Clancy, 36, has pleaded not guilty to charges in the deaths of her three children — Cora (5), Dawson (3), and Callan (8 months) — at their Duxbury, Massachusetts home on Jan. 24, 2023.
  • Prosecutors challenged the closeness of Zeizel's relationship with Clancy and her credibility during cross-examination.
