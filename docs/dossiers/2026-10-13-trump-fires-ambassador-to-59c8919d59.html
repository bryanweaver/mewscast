Consensus Facts

  • Kimberly Guilfoyle served as U.S. ambassador to Greece, based in Athens.
  • Guilfoyle is the ex-fiancée of Donald Trump Jr., the president's eldest son, and a former Fox News personality.
  • On Monday, Oct. 12, 2026, President Trump told reporters he was 'looking at' firing Guilfoyle, saying 'we're not happy.'
  • The Wall Street Journal published a series of reports beginning in late September 2026 alleging improper conduct by Guilfoyle.
  • A central allegation is that Guilfoyle asked Trump donor Eric Deters to pay a roughly $100,000 American Express bill before her Senate confirmation hearing, in exchange for access to the administration.
  • Guilfoyle's lawyers issued a statement (dated around Oct. 12) denying wrongdoing and asserting the money sought from Deters was payment for prior speaking/consulting work, not corruption.
  • Guilfoyle's lawyers rejected allegations of impropriety regarding private-plane travel, saying she sought, obtained and followed State Department travel guidance.
  • Allegations include boasting about Greek business tycoons bankrolling luxury travel, and a claim she told an official the U.S. could topple/undermine the Greek government.
  • Secretary of State Marco Rubio was in Athens with Guilfoyle in early October 2026 and declined/refused to comment publicly on the allegations, berating journalists for asking.
  • Democrats on the House Foreign Affairs and Senate Foreign Relations committees (Rep. Gregory Meeks and Sen. Jeanne Shaheen) called for an investigation into Guilfoyle's conduct.
