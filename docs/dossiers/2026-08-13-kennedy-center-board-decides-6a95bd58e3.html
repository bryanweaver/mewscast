Consensus Facts

  • On Thursday, August 13, 2026, the Kennedy Center's board voted to add President Donald Trump's name to the building's facade.
  • The inscription will read 'Restored and Renovated by President Donald J. Trump,' placed below the Kennedy Center sign.
  • The board also voted to move forward with a plan to partially close the center's main building for up to two years for renovations.
  • The plan keeps a smaller wing known as the REACH open for some events, including possible performances by the National Symphony Orchestra.
  • The actions challenge a May ruling by U.S. District Judge Christopher Cooper, who found the board could not rename the center or add a name without congressional approval and blocked its earlier closure plan.
  • Judge Cooper's ruling was grounded in the finding that the center's governing statute designates it as a memorial to President John F. Kennedy and that only Congress can change its name.
  • Trump's name was removed from the building in June 2026 in compliance with the court order.
  • Rep. Joyce Beatty (D-Ohio), an ex officio board member and a plaintiff in the lawsuit challenging the renaming, condemned the vote as 'a transparent effort to circumvent the Court's ruling, and flies in the face of the statutes that Congress passed.'
  • White House spokesperson Liz Huston said Trump's 'bold leadership' is making the Kennedy Center 'on its way to becoming the finest cultural institution anywhere in the world.'
  • Trump appointed himself chairman of the board last year and filled the board with allies after replacing several trustees.
  • The board meeting was held virtually via Zoom.
  • The vote also included a plan to rename the physical grounds/site as 'President Donald J. Trump Plaza.'
