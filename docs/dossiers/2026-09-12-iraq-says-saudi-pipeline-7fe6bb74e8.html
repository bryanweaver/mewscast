Consensus Facts

  • Iraq said attacks on a key Saudi oil pipeline (the East-West pipeline) were launched from its territory.
  • US President Donald Trump said Iran was 'probably' behind the pipeline attack, telling reporters: 'Well, I think they are. Probably they are.'
  • Trump said his administration has 'had discussions' with the Houthis and quoted them as saying they 'don't want to fight with us' and are 'letting most ships go through.'
  • Trump described Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman as 'a good friend' and said everything is 'going to work out fine and dandy.'
  • At least 76,000 people have fled their homes in Yemen since July, up sharply from 46,000 just days earlier, according to the International Organization for Migration.
  • The escalation in Yemen has raised fears of a return to the civil war that killed about 150,000 people before a 2022 ceasefire.
  • Fighting has intensified between the Iran-backed Houthis and forces aligned with Yemen's Saudi-backed, internationally recognized government.
  • The Houthis' advance near the Bab el-Mandeb strait threatens a key alternative shipping route to the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Lebanese and Israeli negotiators are scheduled to meet in Rome in October for another round of US-brokered direct talks.
  • Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian met with a UAE leader on the sidelines of the BRICS summit in New Delhi.
