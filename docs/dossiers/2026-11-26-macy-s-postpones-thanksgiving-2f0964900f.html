Consensus Facts

  • The Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City has been postponed to Saturday, Nov. 28, with the procession beginning at 8:30 a.m. ET.
  • This is the first time the parade has been postponed due to weather in its history; it was suspended from 1942 to 1944 during World War II.
  • The postponement was driven by a strong coastal storm (nor'easter) bringing heavy rain and high winds to the New York area on Thursday.
  • This year marks the 100th edition of the parade, which was expected to feature a record 100 inflatable balloons.
  • The parade's giant balloons were grounded once before due to weather, in 1971.
  • Macy's and officials stated the decision was made in the interest of safety for participants and spectators, after consultation with the city.
  • Will Coss (also rendered Cross) is identified as the executive producer of the parade and said Macy's was 'heartbroken' over the postponement.
  • New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani said he would not attend Saturday's parade because of a scheduled surgery.
  • The parade's balloons were to be deflated Wednesday night, stored at a facility in New Jersey, and re-inflated for Saturday.
  • NBC planned to air the 2025 parade during Thursday's allotted time slot instead of a live telecast.
  • The parade debuted in 1924, originally conceived as a holiday/Christmas parade to boost store sales.
  • Featured performers slated for the parade included country artist Megan Moroney and singer Teddy Swims.
  • New balloons for the 100th parade included Bluey and a Minion riding a banana.
  • A 1997 incident in which a Cat in the Hat balloon was pushed by wind into a lamppost seriously injured a spectator and prompted changes to safety regulations.
