Consensus Facts

  • US forces struck two Iranian launchers on Larak Island on Sunday, according to US Central Command (CNN, Axios, ABC, CNBC).
  • The strike was the first US military action against Iran in over a month, with the last known strikes occurring in late July (CNN, Axios, ABC, CNBC).
  • CENTCOM said Iranian Revolutionary Guard forces were observed preparing to launch rockets carrying sea mines into the Strait of Hormuz (CNN, Axios, CNBC).
  • Iran's IRGC responded by launching missile (and, per some, drone) strikes on the King Hussein and Al-Azraq US bases in Jordan (CNN, Al Jazeera, ABC, CNBC).
  • Jordan's armed forces said they intercepted eight missiles that entered the kingdom's airspace (CNN, Al Jazeera, Axios, ABC).
  • The IRGC characterized the US strike as an aggression that would be met with punishment/'punishment of the aggressor' (CNN, Al Jazeera, Axios, ABC, CNBC).
  • CENTCOM said it completed clearing sea mines from the strait's international shipping routes last week (CNN, Axios, ABC, CNBC).
  • Trump previously said any ship or boat placing new mines would be immediately and systematically destroyed under a 'zero tolerance' policy (CNN, Axios, ABC).
  • The US-Iran war has now passed the six-month mark, having begun in late February 2026 (ABC, CNBC).
  • US Central Command's spokesperson is identified as Capt. Tim Hawkins (CNN, Axios, CNBC).
