Consensus Facts

  • The Senate passed a stopgap funding bill early Saturday to avert a government shutdown ahead of the midterm elections.
  • The vote was 90-6 in favor of the measure.
  • The bill would fund the government at existing levels through Dec. 11.
  • The bill now goes to the House, which must approve it and send it to President Trump before the Oct. 1 fiscal year deadline to avoid a shutdown.
  • The House previously passed a different short-term funding bill (through Dec. 4).
  • The bill includes a provision, sought by Democrats, aimed at preventing the Trump administration from putting political appointees in charge of approving/reviewing federal grants for political reasons.
  • The Senate worked through a late-night session that stretched into the early morning hours before adjourning for August recess.
  • Lawmakers in both parties were motivated to avoid a shutdown fight ahead of the November election.
