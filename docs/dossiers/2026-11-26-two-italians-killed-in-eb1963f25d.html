Consensus Facts

  • Two Italian nationals were killed in Kuwait on Thursday following a drone attack that officials blamed on Iran.
  • The two Italians were technicians working for defense company Rheinmetall (Rheinmetall Italia), performing maintenance on an air-defence system used by Kuwait's armed forces.
  • Two other people, a Kuwaiti national and a Pakistani national, were wounded by falling shrapnel/debris in the same incident.
  • Kuwait's Defence Ministry said the attack targeted a 'vital site'/'vital installation' and attributed it to 'Iranian aggression,' and that Kuwaiti air defences detected and engaged several hostile drones.
  • Iran denied involvement; Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi called the incident 'suspicious' and a 'precooked recipe for war,' and offered to cooperate with Kuwait and Italy in an investigation.
  • Iran's armed forces spokesperson Abolfazl Shekarchi said no military or offensive operations were conducted by Iran and suggested a 'false flag' operation.
  • Araghchi held phone calls with his Italian counterpart Antonio Tajani (and, per some reports, his Kuwaiti counterpart) to convey Iran's denial.
  • Italy's Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni expressed condolences to the victims' families on X.
  • Italy's Foreign Ministry confirmed the deaths and said the technicians were maintaining air-defence detection equipment.
  • The incident is part of a broader conflict that began with US-Israeli strikes on Iran in late February 2026, in which Gulf states hosting US forces have been targeted.
  • The attack came roughly a week to just over a week after Kuwait said it had repelled Iranian drone attacks on an airbase hosting US forces — the first such strikes in about two months.
