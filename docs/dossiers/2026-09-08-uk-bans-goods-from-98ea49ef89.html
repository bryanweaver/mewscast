Consensus Facts

  • On September 8, 2026, the UK announced a ban on imports of goods produced in Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank.
  • UK Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband announced the measures and stated the British government agrees there is 'ethnic cleansing' of Palestinians in areas of the West Bank perpetrated by 'settler terrorists.'
  • Miliband said there had been 'houses bulldozed, roads and public infrastructure destroyed, families displaced from their homes.'
  • France and Canada announced they would take similar action to ban goods from Israeli settlements.
  • A joint statement from UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham, French President Emmanuel Macron and Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney framed settlement expansion and settler violence as a direct/urgent threat to the two-state solution and said they were acting before it is too late.
  • Israel responded by announcing it would close the British consulate in Jerusalem.
  • Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar condemned the move and announced additional countermeasures, including expelling UK representatives from a joint military center monitoring the Gaza ceasefire, ending UK training of Palestinian Authority security forces in the West Bank, and barring a dozen British lawmakers/citizens from entering Israel.
  • The trade ban is expected to take effect in six to nine months.
  • The move follows the UK's recognition of Palestinian statehood roughly a year earlier.
  • Miliband also announced tougher restrictions on arms sales to Israel.
  • The measures target goods originating specifically from settlements rather than a wider ban on all Israeli products.
  • Multiple additional European countries (including Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Poland, Portugal, Sweden, the Netherlands, Ireland, Belgium, Spain and Norway) have taken or pledged similar steps or support.
