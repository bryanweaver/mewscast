Consensus Facts

  • Hurricane Lala weakened to a tropical storm on Sunday after passing near Hawaii without making a direct landfall (CNN, Al Jazeera, NPR).
  • Lala had strengthened to a Category 1 hurricane on Saturday before weakening (CNN, Al Jazeera, NPR).
  • As a tropical storm, Lala was sustaining top winds of about 70 mph (CNN, NPR).
  • At least one person died in a storm-related car accident, reported in the South Point area of the Big Island (CNN, Al Jazeera, NPR).
  • Approximately 130,000 utility customers lost power across Hawaii (CNN, Al Jazeera, NPR).
  • Hawaii Gov. Josh Green said 19 buildings/roofs were lost in the storm (CNN, Al Jazeera, NPR).
  • At least three hospitals switched to generator power, per Gov. Josh Green (Al Jazeera, NPR).
  • Total rainfall of nearly three feet (one meter / up to about 35 inches) was expected on higher windward slopes of the Big Island (CNN, Al Jazeera, NPR).
  • The National Hurricane Center warned of life-threatening flooding and mudslides, especially in steep terrain (CNN, Al Jazeera, NPR).

Al Jazeera: States the Big Island 'has not suffered a direct hurricane landfall in 155 years.'
NPR: Does not assign a specific historical timeframe; simply notes the storm skirted Hawaii without landfall.
Deaths and severity of impact tone
CNN: Leads with '1 dead, thousands without power' and emphasizes tens of thousands losing power, roofs lost, and 30+ inches of rain already fallen on the Big Island.
Al Jazeera: Emphasizes the storm 'just escaping primary impact zones' while still noting one death and heavy damage.
NPR: Emphasizes the storm 'wasn't done punishing the Big Island' and details ongoing rescue and forecast risks.
Rainfall totals already recorded vs. forecast
CNN: Reports the Big Island 'got over 30 inches of rain' (past tense, already fallen).
NPR: Frames the ~35 inches as a forecast total by the time the storm is gone ('will have been hit with as much as 35 inches').
Al Jazeera: Frames up to 90cm (35 inches) as an NHC forecast for some areas.
