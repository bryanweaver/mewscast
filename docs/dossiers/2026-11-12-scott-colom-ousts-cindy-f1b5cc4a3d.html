Consensus Facts

  • Democrat Scott Colom defeated Republican incumbent Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith in the Mississippi US Senate race (BBC, Axios bodies).
  • Colom becomes the first Black senator from Mississippi since Reconstruction (BBC, Axios bodies; BBC frames it as the first Black person from Mississippi elected to the US Senate in more than 140 years).
  • Colom is the first Democrat elected to Mississippi's Senate delegation since 1982, ending a decades-long Republican winning streak (BBC, Axios bodies).
  • Hyde-Smith posted a video message to X conceding and congratulating Colom, saying 'While this is not the outcome we worked for or hoped for, I respect the decision of Mississippi voters' (BBC, Axios bodies).
  • The win expands Democrats' Senate majority to 55 seats against 44 Republicans (BBC, Axios bodies).
  • The victory came in a state Trump carried in 2024 by more than 22 points (BBC says 22+, Axios says 23).
  • Colom is a 43-year-old local prosecutor/district attorney from Columbus, Mississippi (BBC, Axios bodies).
  • The race was extraordinarily close, decided by a narrow margin out of roughly a million votes cast (BBC body; Axios body references recount worries).
  • Democrats flipped Senate seats in multiple Republican strongholds including Texas and South Carolina during these midterms (BBC, Axios bodies).
