Consensus Facts

  • On Friday, October 16, 2026, Israeli settlers attacked Palestinians harvesting olives near the village of Abu Falah, northeast of Ramallah in the occupied West Bank (Al Jazeera, CBS News, CNN, BBC).
  • Heidi Levine, a veteran American photojournalist on assignment for The Washington Post, was knocked to the ground and repeatedly kicked during the attack (Al Jazeera, CBS News, CNN, BBC).
  • Levine was wearing protective gear marked 'PRESS' and/or a helmet when she was attacked, and her camera equipment and phone were taken (Al Jazeera, CBS News, CNN).
  • Levine was taken to a hospital in Ramallah for treatment, with wounds to her hand and leg (Al Jazeera, CBS News, CNN).
  • The attackers were described as masked settlers, numbering around a dozen to roughly 20, who descended a hill from an Israeli settlement outpost (Al Jazeera, CBS News, CNN, BBC).
  • Settlers set olive trees and/or vehicles on fire and hurled stones, including with slingshots (Al Jazeera, CBS News, CNN, BBC).
  • The Washington Post issued a statement calling it an 'unwarranted attack on a journalist doing her job' and urging a thorough investigation (Al Jazeera, CBS News, CNN).
  • Israeli police and the military said they opened investigations into the incident (CBS News, CNN).
  • A U.S. Embassy spokesperson condemned 'criminal violence by any party in the West Bank' and called for investigations, arrests, and convictions (CBS News, CNN).
  • Additional settler attacks on olive pickers occurred the same day near the villages of Burqa and Turmus Aya/Ayya (Al Jazeera, CBS News).
  • The Palestine Red Crescent Society treated multiple Palestinians for injuries at the scene (CBS News, CNN).
  • The attack is part of a broader surge of settler violence during the Palestinian olive harvest, which multiple outlets and the UN describe as reaching record levels (Al Jazeera, CBS News, CNN, BBC).
