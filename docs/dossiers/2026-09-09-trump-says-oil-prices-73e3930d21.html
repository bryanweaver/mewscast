Consensus Facts

  • President Donald Trump said on Sept. 9, 2026, that oil and gas prices that spiked because of the Iran war likely won't come down until after the U.S. midterm elections, predicting 'Right after the election, oil prices are going to be tumbling downward.'
  • Trump predicted the Iran war will end 'immediately after the election,' arguing Iran 'can't hold out any longer.'
  • Trump claimed Iran is 'desperate to try and affect the election, so that we can get a nice weak group of people in there, and leave them alone and let them have their nuclear weapon.'
  • Trump made the comments to reporters before departing Joint Base Andrews for the Republican Party's midterm convention in Dallas.
  • The Iran war is in its seventh month with no clear resolution in sight, after Trump initially predicted it would last only weeks.
  • The U.S. military reported destroying Iranian oil tankers over the past week in response to attempted Iranian missile attacks on U.S. Navy warships.
  • Trump previously described the conflict as 'small potatoes,' and Vice President JD Vance last week declined to characterize it as a war.
  • Trump tempered his prediction by adding that gas dropping below $2 a gallon would 'take a little bit longer than the midterm(s).'
  • The midterm elections are roughly 55 days away / nearly two months out from the Sept. 9 comments (election referenced as Nov. 3 by AP).
