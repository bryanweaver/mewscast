Consensus Facts

  • A major earthquake struck Kumamoto prefecture on Japan's southern main island of Kyushu on the afternoon of Tuesday, July 28, 2026, at around 4:27 p.m. local time (per AP, NBC News, USA Today).
  • The Japan Meteorological Agency gave the earthquake a preliminary magnitude of 7.1 (per AP, USA Today, NBC News).
  • A tsunami advisory/warning was issued after the quake and was later lifted within about two hours (per AP, USA Today, NBC News).
  • People were trapped at the Aeon Mall in Kashima Town, Kumamoto prefecture, after an explosion was reported there following the quake (per AP, USA Today, NBC News).
  • At least 50 people were injured/taken to hospital according to authorities and local news reports (per USA Today, NBC News, CNN citing NHK).
  • Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi confirmed reports of injuries, damage to roads, bridges and buildings, plus blackouts and fires (per AP, NBC News, USA Today).
  • Kumamoto Castle's stone walls sustained damage (per AP, USA Today, NBC News).
  • A chimney at Nippon Paper Industries' Yatsushiro mill partially collapsed and a bridge was damaged (per USA Today, NBC News; AP references a partially collapsed bridge in Yatsushiro).
