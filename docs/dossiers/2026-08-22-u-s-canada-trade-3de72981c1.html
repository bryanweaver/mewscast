Consensus Facts

  • U.S.-Canada trade negotiations collapsed late Friday, August 22, 2026.
  • New 50% tariffs on Canadian goods will take effect following the breakdown.
  • Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney said Canada will 'match those tariffs dollar for dollar to protect our workers and businesses.'
  • Carney announced he was suspending trade negotiations with the U.S.
  • U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said Canada declined to finalize the deal under previously agreed terms and made new last-minute demands.
  • Carney countered that it was the U.S. that made last-minute changes to the proposed terms.
  • The U.S. had earlier this week postponed the tariffs after the two sides indicated a deal was close.
  • Both outlets attribute the collapse to a dispute over last-minute changes to terms, with each side blaming the other.
