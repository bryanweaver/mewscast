Consensus Facts

  • The US House voted 211-204 on Tuesday to refer articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the House Judiciary Committee, a procedural move that effectively kills the impeachment effort in the current Congress.
  • Eleven House Democrats were absent from the vote; their absence was decisive, as their votes could have changed the outcome.
  • The impeachment resolution was introduced by Republican Rep. Thomas Massie of Kentucky, who was the only Republican to oppose the motion to refer.
  • Seven House Republicans were also absent from the vote.
  • The 11 absent Democrats were Julia Brownley, Lou Correa, Laura Gillen, Steven Horsford, Jonathan Jackson, Nancy Pelosi, Brittany Pettersen, Melanie Stansbury, Haley Stevens, Marilyn Strickland, and Frederica Wilson.
  • Several absent Democrats blamed a traffic incident or police motorcade/escort near the Capitol for missing the vote.
  • Nancy Pelosi was in San Francisco/California for a Veterans Day event and did not attend; her office said she supports impeaching Hegseth and would have voted no.
  • Lou Correa cited a medical procedure that prevented him from traveling to Washington.
  • The current (119th) Congress ends on January 3, after which Democrats take control; the referral means the articles are unlikely to return to the floor before then.
  • Some absent Democrats filed statements of their intended 'no' vote in the Congressional Record, which does not change the official vote tally.
  • Haley Stevens had lost support from Democratic colleagues after endorsing Michigan Republican Mike Rogers for Senate over Democratic candidate Abdul El-Sayed.
