Consensus Facts

  • The Norte Fire ignited around midday Friday, Oct. 16, 2026, in the San Clemente / San Juan Capistrano area of Orange County, California (BBC, ABC News US, CNN).
  • The Norte Fire had grown to at least 570 acres with 5% containment as of Saturday morning (BBC, ABC News US, CNN).
  • At least six homes were damaged or destroyed by the Norte Fire (BBC, ABC News US, CNN).
  • Evacuation orders were in effect for parts of Orange County due to the Norte Fire (BBC, ABC News US, CNN).
  • The fires are being driven by fierce Santa Ana winds, high heat, and dry conditions, with the National Weather Service issuing red flag warnings (BBC, ABC News US, CNN).
  • Division 3 Fire Chief Mark Backoff attributed the fire's rapid growth to weather and topography (CNN, ABC News US; Backoff also quoted by BBC).
  • The Bryson Fire in Malibu (Los Angeles County) burned roughly 147-150 acres and was 52% contained, with over 400 firefighters assigned (BBC, CNN).
  • The Henderson Fire near Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton in San Diego County ignited Thursday and prompted closure of a portion of Interstate 5 (BBC, CNN; ABC News US references the Henderson Fire at Camp Pendleton).
  • Several schools in the Capistrano Unified School District were dismissed early Friday 'out of an abundance of caution' (ABC News US, CNN).
