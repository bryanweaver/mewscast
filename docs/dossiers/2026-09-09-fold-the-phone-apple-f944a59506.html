Consensus Facts

  • Apple unveiled its first foldable iPhone, called the iPhone Duo, at its 'Surprise and Shine' event at its Cupertino, California headquarters on Wednesday, September 9, 2026.
  • The iPhone Duo starts at a base price of $1,999.
  • The device opens like a book, with a smaller outer screen when folded and a larger internal screen when unfolded.
  • The device measures 7.6 inches on the internal display when unfolded (NPR and NBC News both cite 7.6 inches; ABC describes it as one continuous screen).
  • The iPhone Duo will be available in stores on October 23 (NPR, NBC, ABC), with preorders beginning October 16 (NBC and ABC).
  • The Duo comes in two colors, described as 'star white' and 'night sky' (NBC and ABC).
  • The announcement was made by Apple's new CEO John Ternus, who succeeded Tim Cook, in Ternus's first keynote as CEO.
  • Apple also announced the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max at the same event, with incremental upgrades and AI/Siri features.
  • Ternus used the phrase 'one more thing,' a line associated with Steve Jobs, to introduce the foldable device (NPR and NBC).
  • Ternus characterized rivals' foldables as feeling like 'two phones awkwardly stuck together' (NBC and ABC quote this line).
  • Foldable phones are an existing category dominated for years by competitors such as Samsung and Huawei; Apple is entering as a follower.
  • The device is described as roughly passport-sized when folded (NPR and NBC).
