Consensus Facts

  • On July 8, 2026, President Donald Trump departed the NATO summit in Ankara, Turkey, and was reportedly secretly transferred off Air Force One via an airport catering truck/container to a separate military aircraft (a C-32A, a military Boeing 757), with the original Air Force One flying as a decoy carrying press and some White House staff.
  • The plane switch was first reported by The Washington Post on Monday (Aug. 10), and subsequently reported by The New York Times; the reported reason was an Iranian assassination threat.
  • Trump flew on the alternate military aircraft from Turkey to RAF Mildenhall in Suffolk, England.
  • On Tuesday, Aug. 11, 2026, speaking to reporters at Joint Base Andrews after returning from Ohio, Trump confirmed the switch and attributed the decision to the Secret Service and the military, saying 'I go by Secret Service and the military' and that 'they wanted me to do it, so I do it.'
  • Trump downplayed knowledge of a specific threat, saying he receives many threats and did not ask much about it.
  • Neither the Secret Service nor the White House confirmed or denied the specifics; the White House said only that it uses every tool available to protect the president.
