Consensus Facts

  • Wisconsin holds its Democratic primary for governor on Tuesday, August 11, 2026.
  • Francesca Hong, a 37-year-old democratic socialist, state lawmaker, and former chef/restaurant owner, has led in public polling throughout much of the primary.
  • Milwaukee County Executive David Crowley, backed by outgoing Gov. Tony Evers, has emerged as the leading more-moderate/centrist alternative to Hong.
  • Crowley dropped out of the race and then re-entered days later, with Evers' backing.
  • Gov. Tony Evers is not seeking a third term, opening the Democratic nomination.
  • Former Lt. Gov. Mandela Barnes and Lt. Gov. Sara Rodriguez entered the race but both dropped out; Rodriguez cited campaign finance filing inaccuracies and having less money than claimed.
  • Rep. Tom Tiffany, a member of the hard-right House Freedom Caucus endorsed by President Trump, is all but certain to win the Republican nomination.
  • Hong has faced scrutiny over past social media posts, including calls to defund the police and to 'cancel Thanksgiving,' and has distanced herself from some of them.
  • Hong's platform includes a moratorium/hold on data centers, higher taxes on the wealthy, and expanded social programs.
  • Wisconsin is a swing state whose statewide races are typically decided by margins of one percentage point or less; Trump won it in 2024 by less than a point (roughly 30,000 votes).
  • Hong's rise is framed as part of a broader national wave of progressive/democratic socialist primary victories, including Abdul El-Sayed's win the prior week in Michigan's Democratic Senate primary.
  • Minnesota also holds primaries Tuesday, including a competitive Democratic U.S. Senate primary between Lt. Gov. Peggy Flanagan and Rep. Angie Craig.
