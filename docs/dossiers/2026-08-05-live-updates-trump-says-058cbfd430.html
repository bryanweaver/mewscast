Disagreements

Whether the U.S. is directly negotiating with Iran
BBC News: Iran says it is negotiating with Oman but NOT the U.S., and has no plans to; Trump insisted talks were taking place and called Iran 'duplicitous.'
CNBC: Treasury Secretary Bessent said 'We are in talks with the Iranians'; a Pakistani official described the U.S. as involved 'indirectly,' with Pakistan mediating between Iran and Oman.
CBS News: Frames the talks as direct Iran-Oman discussions, with the U.S. and Iran to return to the table under June MOU terms if approved.
Whether the proposed deal includes transit fees/tolls
CBS News (Strait of Hormuz deal live page): The proposal under negotiation would have NO tolls or service fees for transiting ships.
CBS News (Rubio live page, earlier update): The proposal includes a 'service fee' with revenue split between Iran and Oman.
Reuters (headline only): Headline states the proposed deal would give Iran control of inbound traffic; does not address fees in retrievable text.
Status of U.S. missile stockpiles
CBS News: Two sources say the U.S. has used 'virtually all' of its ATACMS and Precision Strike Missiles; Pentagon spokesman and Sec. Hegseth dismiss the reporting.
CNBC: Reuters and CNN reported severe depletion (including ~80% of THAAD interceptors); White House, Pentagon, and Trump insist there are 'more than enough' munitions.
Current openness of the Strait of Hormuz
CNBC: CENTCOM says the southern route through Omani waters 'remains free and open,' having assisted 1,000+ vessels; Trump says the strait 'is going to be open very soon.'
CBS News: Notes the state of commercial traffic remains 'murky'; Iran and Oman allege they control the waterway; cites CENTCOM's open-southern-route claim.
BBC News: Cites Kpler vessel-tracking data showing commercial shipping has slowed, with a ~28% drop in crossings between July 30 and Aug. 2.
Timeline of the announced talks
BBC News: Reports Trump said new talks would begin Monday and that he called off 'massive' strikes; article dated '2 days ago,' reflecting an earlier phase.
Associated Press / CNBC / CBS News: Report Trump's Aug. 4-5 statements that a deal could come Wednesday or Thursday.
