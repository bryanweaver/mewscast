Consensus Facts

  • Democrats won control of both chambers of the US Congress in the 2026 midterm elections, flipping at least seven Senate seats (ABC News, The Guardian, Time, The Independent).
  • James Talarico defeated Ken Paxton to become the first Democrat to win a US Senate seat in Texas since 1988 (ABC News via results, BBC, The Guardian, Time).
  • Adam Hamilton, a church pastor, defeated incumbent Republican Senator Roger Marshall in Kansas — the first Democratic Senate win in the state since 1932/94 years (BBC, The Guardian).
  • The Senate tally stood at 54 Democrats and 44 Republicans at the time of reporting (ABC News, The Independent).
  • Senate races in Mississippi (Scott Colom vs. Cindy Hyde-Smith) and Alaska (Mary Peltola vs. Dan Sullivan) remained too close to call, with margins of roughly 1,100 and 600 votes respectively (ABC News, Time, The Independent).
  • Democrats flipped Senate seats in deep-red states including Iowa, Kansas, North Carolina, South Carolina and Texas (The Guardian, Time).
  • Trump claimed he 'wasn't on the Ballot' but 'would have won IN A LANDSLIDE,' reversing weeks of urging voters to treat him as a candidate (The Guardian articles 5 and 7).
  • Many voters cited the rising cost of living and the Iran war as reasons for voting against Republicans (ABC News, BBC, The Guardian, Time, The Independent).
  • Vice President JD Vance blamed the Republican losses primarily on Trump's war with Iran (The Guardian, Time).
  • House Speaker Mike Johnson will not seek re-election as Republican leader (The Guardian, Time).
  • Gina Hinojosa ousted incumbent Republican Governor Greg Abbott to become the first Latina and first Democratic governor of Texas in decades (BBC, The Guardian).
