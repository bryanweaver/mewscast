Consensus Facts

  • Democrat Chuck Hubbard defeated longtime Republican Rep. Virginia Foxx in North Carolina's 5th Congressional District (NBC News, Al Jazeera, CBS News, ABC News, Washington Post).
  • The win gives Democrats at least 258 House seats, their largest majority since 1992, when the party also won 258 seats (NBC News, Al Jazeera, CBS News, ABC News, Washington Post).
  • This is described as the largest House majority either party has held so far this century (NBC News, CBS News, Washington Post, Al Jazeera).
  • Foxx requested a recount after Election Day and conceded on Sunday, calling Hubbard to concede the race (NBC News, CBS News, ABC News).
  • The race was decided by a razor-thin margin — fewer than 300 to 330 votes, with Hubbard leading roughly 48.7% to 48.6% (Al Jazeera, ABC News).
  • Four House races remain uncalled, meaning Democrats could still expand their majority (NBC News, Al Jazeera, ABC News).
  • Hubbard is a former journalist who previously ran against Foxx in 2024 and lost (CBS News, ABC News, Washington Post).
  • Virginia Foxx chairs the House Rules Committee and was first elected to the U.S. House in 2004, serving since 2005 (NBC News, Al Jazeera, CBS News, ABC News).
  • The race had been rated 'likely Republican' by the Cook Political Report heading into Election Night (CBS News, Washington Post).
  • Hubbard's concession statement thanked Foxx for 'her decades of service and countless sacrifices' (CBS News, ABC News).
  • Trump won Foxx's district by a wide margin (nearly 18 points per NBC) in 2024, underscoring the scale of the flip (NBC News, implied by CBS News's 'flipped a seat Foxx held').
  • The AP projected/called the race in Hubbard's favor on Sunday (Al Jazeera, ABC News).
