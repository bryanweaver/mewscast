  • Jordan currently chairs the House Judiciary Committee and is a longtime ally of President Donald Trump.
  • The leadership change followed a historic Democratic 'blue wave' in the midterms that flipped control to Democrats and prompted Speaker Mike Johnson to step aside from leadership.
