Consensus Facts

  • German Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt described the Leipzig incident as a 'new quality/level of danger' and characterized it as a professional hybrid threat scenario.
  • The Leipzig drone was found near/between Ukrainian Antonov cargo aircraft on the tarmac.
  • The explosive on the drone was fist-sized and believed to be Semtex.
  • Leipzig/Halle Airport is a major cargo hub used for military support to Ukraine and hosts NATO-related logistics.
  • German and European security experts have pointed to Russia as the most likely origin of the Leipzig threat, though the German government stopped short of officially attributing it.
  • A DHL aircraft leaving Leipzig due to the airport closure struck an object believed to be a second drone and was diverted to Hannover with minor/nose damage.
  • Germany recorded more than 1,000 suspicious drone overflights in 2025.
