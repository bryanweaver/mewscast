Consensus Facts

  • Democrats won control of both the House and Senate in the November 3, 2026 US midterm elections, ending unified Republican control of Congress (reported in full by Associated Press, Al Jazeera English, and CNBC).
  • Trump publicly acknowledged that the war on Iran impacted the midterm results, while saying the action was necessary (Associated Press, Al Jazeera English, and CNBC).
  • The war on Iran — a joint US-Israel campaign — began in late February 2026 without congressional authorization and has driven up gas, fuel, food, and inflation costs that frustrated voters (Associated Press, Al Jazeera English, and CNBC; Al Jazeera and CNBC cite the start as Feb. 28).
