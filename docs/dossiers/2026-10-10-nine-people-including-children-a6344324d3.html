Consensus Facts

  • A mass shooting occurred at a home near 7th and Perry Streets in Erie, Pennsylvania, on the evening of Friday, Oct. 9, 2026.
  • Officers responded at approximately 4:50–5:00 p.m. after reports of gunfire; ABC News attributes this to a ShotSpotter alert.
  • The suspected shooter is dead, and authorities believe he died of a self-inflicted gunshot wound.
  • Erie Police Chief Rick Lorah described the scene as among the most difficult and heartbreaking of his nearly three-decade (28-plus year) career.
  • Authorities said there is no ongoing threat to the public and that a motive has not yet been established.
  • Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro said he spoke with Erie Mayor Daria Devlin and offered his administration's 'full support,' writing 'My heart breaks for this family' and that he and Lori are praying for the loved ones and the Erie community.
  • Officials said all deaths occurred before officers arrived, and no additional shots were fired between officers' arrival and their entry into the home.
  • Victims had not been publicly identified at the time of reporting, and a follow-up news conference was scheduled for 4 p.m. Saturday, Oct. 10.
  • Erie is roughly 117–128 miles north of Pittsburgh.
