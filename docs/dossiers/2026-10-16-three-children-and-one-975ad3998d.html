Consensus Facts

  • A shooting occurred at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday, Oct. 15, 2026.
  • A 16-year-old male Bend resident was the shooter.
  • Three children — ages 5, 8 and 11 — and one teacher were killed.
  • A 7-year-old child and a 54-year-old teacher were taken to the hospital with critical injuries.
  • The shooter forced his way into the school by firing at locked doors with a long gun.
  • The incident began shortly before 1 p.m. local time when police received a 911 call from a driver who reported being shot at outside the school.
  • A community member/bystander saw the shooter enter the school, pursued him inside, and fatally shot him.
  • Police arrived on scene within minutes and confirmed the suspect was dead before evacuating the school.
  • The suspect's identity was not publicly released, and officials said they were still investigating whether he had ties to the school.
  • Bend Police Chief Mike Krantz spoke at an evening news conference and is the lead investigative agency.
  • Oregon Gov. Tina Kotek praised the community member who 'heroically intervened' and acknowledged the emotional burden on them.
  • The FBI, under Director Kash Patel, assisted local authorities in the investigation.
  • A family reunification site was set up at Journey Church in Bend.
  • All Bend-La Pine Schools classes and activities were canceled for Friday.
