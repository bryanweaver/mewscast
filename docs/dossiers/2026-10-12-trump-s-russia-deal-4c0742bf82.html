Consensus Facts

  • President Trump announced Friday that Russia would supply diesel fuel to U.S. and global markets; Axios and NBC News both report this.

  • Sen. Thom Tillis (R-N.C.) said on NBC News' 'Meet the Press' that Trump's deal may violate the law requiring the White House to notify Congress / may not be following the law; reported in both Axios and NBC News.
