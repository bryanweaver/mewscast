Consensus Facts

  • President Donald Trump announced on Truth Social on Saturday that he had offered UFC president and CEO Dana White the job of White House press secretary (reported in full body by NBC News, CNBC, and Variety).
  • Trump's post included the phrase: 'I am pleased to announce that the Great Dana White, a violent Fighter and Warrior, has been offered the job of Press Secretary of the United States' (quoted by NBC News, CNBC, and Variety).
  • Trump wrote that the offer extended to assistants, some of whom are 'not from our Country,' and that these people are needed 'to defend us against the Lunatic Left' (reported by CNBC and Variety).
  • Trump and Dana White have a longstanding friendship, and White helped organize/host a UFC fight on the White House grounds earlier in 2026 (reported by NBC News and CNBC).
  • The offer follows Friday's announcement that deputy chief of staff Stephen Miller would be promoted to White House chief of staff, replacing Susie Wiles (reported by NBC News and CNBC).
  • The job offer comes days after Democrats won majority control of the House and Senate in the midterm elections (reported by NBC News and CNBC).
  • Trump previously speculated/was asked about considering rapper Nicki Minaj for the press secretary role (reported by CNBC and Variety).
