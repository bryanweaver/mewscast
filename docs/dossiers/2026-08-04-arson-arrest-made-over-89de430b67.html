Consensus Facts

  • A 37-year-old man named Aaron F. Farinacci was arrested Monday, August 3, 2026, in connection with the Old Trails Fire in Spokane County, Washington.
  • Farinacci was booked on a preliminary charge of first-degree arson and held on a $1 million bond.
  • The investigation began after a witness reported seeing a man kneeling near grass at the site where the fire started, and later observed smoke coming from the area.
  • Farinacci was found in possession of waterproof matches and a butane lighter when detained by law enforcement, according to Spokane County Sheriff John Nowels.
  • Three major fires — Old Trails, Fairview, and Autumn Lane — have been burning in the Spokane area since Saturday.
  • More than 700 structures/buildings in the Spokane area have been destroyed.
  • Roughly 60,000 to 67,000 people have been evacuated in Spokane County.
  • No deaths or injuries have been confirmed.
  • Authorities were attempting to contact 14 people who had not been reachable but who were not considered missing.
  • Washington Gov. Bob Ferguson described the event as a 'nightmare situation' and 'likely the worst natural disaster in Spokane history,' and said the arson allegation makes the situation 'more heartbreaking.'
  • There are 16 to 17 major wildfires burning across Washington state.
  • Spokane resident Anna Andreachi, who lost her home, said 'All of our memories are gone. My dad just died, and all of his stuff was still in there too.'
  • Joe Cvancara saved his home by digging a fire line and described feeling 'survivor's guilt,' pledging to open his home to neighbors.
  • The fires remain uncontained (0% containment), and dangerous heat and wind conditions are forecast to return midweek.
