Consensus Facts

  • CIA Director John Ratcliffe traveled to Moscow on Tuesday for unannounced talks (reported by The Washington Post, BBC, NBC News, CNN, and ABC News).
  • Neither the US nor Russia confirmed the nature of the visit or whom Ratcliffe was meeting; the CIA declined to comment (Washington Post, BBC, NBC News, CNN).
  • This is Ratcliffe's first known trip to Moscow as CIA director (BBC, NBC News, CNN).
  • Publicly available flight tracking data from Flightradar24 captured a US government/military transport aircraft traveling from Joint Base Andrews in Maryland toward Moscow, routing through Riga, Latvia (Washington Post, BBC, NBC News, CNN).
  • The aircraft landed at Moscow's Vnukovo International Airport in the morning and departed later the same day (BBC, NBC News, CNN).
  • The trip comes as negotiations to end the Russia-Ukraine war have stalled and as Ukraine has stepped up strikes deep inside Russia (Washington Post, BBC, CNN).
  • The US told/asked Ukraine to halt drone and missile strikes in Moscow ahead of the visit, informing Kyiv that a senior US delegation would be traveling to Russia (BBC, CNN).
  • CBS News was first to report on Ratcliffe's presence in Russia (BBC, NBC News, CNN).
  • CIA directors have historically acted as presidential envoys with Russia, as when then-Director William Burns relayed warnings before the 2022 invasion (referenced by NBC News; BBC notes Ratcliffe's prior role in prisoner-release negotiations).
