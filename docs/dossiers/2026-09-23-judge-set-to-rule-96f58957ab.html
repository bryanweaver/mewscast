Consensus Facts

  • U.S. District Judge Timothy Kelly held an emergency hearing on Wednesday, Sept. 23, 2026, over the Trump administration's ban of CNN, MS NOW and Politico from White House grounds.
  • Kelly did not rule from the bench and said he would issue a decision as soon as possible.
  • Kelly was nominated/appointed by Trump in 2017 and previously ordered the restoration of CNN reporter Jim Acosta's White House credentials during Trump's first term.
  • The three outlets sought a temporary restraining order to immediately reinstate their White House access while their legal challenge proceeds.
  • The news outlets argue the ban is viewpoint discrimination and a violation of their First Amendment rights.
  • Justice Department attorney Michael Velchik argued that 'Access to the White House is a privilege, not a right.'
  • Theodore Boutrous Jr. represented the news organizations at the hearing.
  • Kelly indicated the government did not appear to follow binding legal precedent, saying the process 'wasn't followed here.'
  • The administration filed letters citing 'reporting incidents,' including CNN reporting on East Wing bunker construction details, MS NOW coverage related to a leak investigation / Iran war munitions, and Politico's reporting on White House ballroom funding involving Secret Service security improvements.
  • The administration invoked national security concerns and cited the imminent state visit of Chinese President Xi Jinping to Washington.
  • The lawsuit was filed on Monday (Sept. 21/22, 2026) and the White House sent letters to the outlets on Tuesday.
