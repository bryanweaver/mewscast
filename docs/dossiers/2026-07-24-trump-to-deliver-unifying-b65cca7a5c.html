Consensus Facts

  • The White House Correspondents' Association Dinner, disrupted in April by a shooting, is being reheld on Friday, July 24, 2026.
  • President Donald Trump is scheduled to attend and deliver remarks at the rescheduled dinner.
  • The rescheduled dinner is being held at the Waldorf Astoria (formerly the Trump International Hotel) rather than the Washington Hilton, which had hosted the event for decades.
  • The new venue is smaller and more secure, described by organizers as a more 'intimate' gathering.
  • The April event was disrupted when a gunman, identified as Cole Tomas Allen, 31, of California, rushed a security checkpoint in an alleged attempt to assassinate the president.
  • Allen exchanged gunfire with law enforcement and has pleaded not guilty to charges including attempting to assassinate the president and assaulting a federal officer with a deadly weapon.
  • A Secret Service officer, Victor Gonzales, was shot but struck in his protective vest and continued responding to the threat; he will receive the President's Award for Exceptional Service at the dinner.
  • WHCA president Weijia Jiang (a CBS News reporter) framed the rescheduling as 'a statement that violence has no place in American life and a free press will not be intimidated into silence.'
  • More than 500 journalists and press freedom groups signed a letter urging the WHCA to condemn the administration's attacks on the media, calling it 'hypocritical to celebrate the First Amendment in front of the man who relentlessly attacks it.'
  • Trump did not deliver his planned April speech and has publicly weighed whether to give the same 'nasty' remarks he originally prepared.
  • The Justice Department recently subpoenaed New York Times journalists over reporting on the Qatari-gifted Air Force One jet, then withdrew those subpoenas shortly before the dinner.
  • First lady Melania Trump, Vice President JD Vance, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio attended the April dinner but will not attend the rescheduled one.
  • The dinner marks Trump's first attendance at the WHCA dinner in either of his presidential terms, after he declined to attend throughout his first term.
  • White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt characterized the upcoming speech and the president's participation on behalf of the White House.
