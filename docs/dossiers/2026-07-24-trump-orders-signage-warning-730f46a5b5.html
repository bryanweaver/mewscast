Consensus Facts

  • On Friday, July 24, 2026, President Donald Trump signed an executive order directing that signage be installed outside the Smithsonian's National Museum of American History to warn visitors about 'inaccurate information' the administration says is presented in the museum.
  • The order directs the Department of the Interior / National Park Service (which controls the sidewalks and walkways leading to the museum) to install the temporary signage.
  • The signage will inform visitors of the findings of a White House report and direct them to 'locations and resources for accurate information' about American history.
  • The order states the Museum's exhibits should be 'renovated consistent with the findings' of the report.
  • The order references and builds on a White House report released July 4, 2026, that accused the museum's leadership of 'extreme political activism' and of failing to treat American history as a shared national inheritance.
  • Trump's order quotes the report's claim that Smithsonian leadership 'views American history as a prime tool to advance ideas of social justice and the radical transformation of our society.'
  • The order cites Trump's earlier March 2025 executive order that targeted 'improper, divisive, or anti-American ideology' at the Smithsonian.
  • The order tasks Interior Secretary Doug Burgum, and additional officials, with using 'all available authorities' to promote the order's policy.
  • The Smithsonian declined to comment on Friday's order.
  • The order follows congressional hearings days earlier in which museum director Anthea Hartig was questioned by Republican lawmakers who accused the museum of presenting history from a leftist or 'woke' viewpoint.
