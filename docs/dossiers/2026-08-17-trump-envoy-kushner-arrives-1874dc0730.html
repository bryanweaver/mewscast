Consensus Facts

  • Jared Kushner, President Donald Trump's son-in-law and envoy, held a rare meeting with a Hamas delegation led by Khalil al-Hayya in Egypt on Sunday, Aug. 16, 2026 (NBC News, BBC, NPR, Washington Post).
  • Kushner met with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Monday, Aug. 17, 2026, in a push to advance the US-backed Gaza peace plan (NBC News, BBC, NPR).
  • The plan at issue is a 15-point, US-backed road map requiring Hamas disarmament and Israeli troop withdrawal from Gaza (NBC News, BBC, NPR).
  • Netanyahu last week rejected the 15-point plan in a rare public break with the Trump administration, saying Israel will not withdraw until Hamas is fully disarmed (NBC News, BBC, NPR, Washington Post).
  • Kushner was accompanied by Board of Peace envoys Nickolay Mladenov and former British Prime Minister Tony Blair (NBC News, BBC, NPR).
  • Egypt, Qatar and Turkey participated as mediators in the Hamas meeting (BBC, NPR).
  • Kushner also met Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi on Sunday (NBC News, BBC, Washington Post).
  • A joint statement by regional powers — including Egypt, UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Turkey, Pakistan and Indonesia — condemned Israel's rejection of the road map and said Israel bears responsibility for obstructing peace efforts (NBC News, BBC, NPR).
  • Far-right Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir, in a podcast interview with released hostage Rom Braslavski, called for nightly 'targeted' killings of '30 or 40' people in Gaza and said some there 'are not even people' (NBC News, BBC).
  • The ceasefire took effect Oct. 10, 2025, and remains in place, but Israel has conducted near-daily strikes since; more than 1,200 people have been killed in Gaza since the ceasefire began, according to the Hamas-run health ministry (NBC News, BBC).
  • Netanyahu faces an Israeli general election (dated Oct. 27 by NPR) amid pressure from his far-right coalition partners (NBC News, BBC, NPR).
