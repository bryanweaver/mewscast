Consensus Facts

  • A Ukrainian drone attack on St. Petersburg overnight into Sunday, Oct. 18, 2026, killed one person and wounded five, according to local Gov. Alexander Beglov.
  • The strike sparked fires at the St. Petersburg Oil Terminal and the Ruchi/Ruchyi oil depot.
  • A logistics center belonging to Russian retailer Ozon and a data center operated by Russian tech company VK/VKontakte were also targeted; both companies confirmed being hit.
  • Russia's Defense Ministry said its forces downed 1,015 Ukrainian drones over Russian territory overnight.
  • Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Russia had attacked Ukraine's energy, oil and gas sectors 76 times in the week since Trump announced an 'energy ceasefire.'
  • Ukraine paused its strikes on Russian oil facilities for roughly three days after Trump's Oct. 11 'energy ceasefire' announcement, then resumed them earlier in the week.
  • On Oct. 11, Trump announced that Russia and Ukraine had agreed to an 'energy ceasefire,' but offered no details and neither side fully confirmed it.
  • Trump signed an executive order Friday night waiving planned sanctions against Russia, citing U.S. national interests, drawing criticism including from some Republicans.
  • Speaking on NBC's 'Meet the Press' on Sunday, Zelensky said waiving sanctions would strengthen Putin's war effort and that siding with Moscow would be a 'historic mistake.'
  • Zelensky said U.S.-Ukraine intelligence sharing had been blocked for a period and has since resumed.
  • Zelensky said he spoke with U.S. envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner on Sunday about next diplomatic steps.
  • Russia and Ukraine have been escalating strikes on each other's energy infrastructure heading into winter.
