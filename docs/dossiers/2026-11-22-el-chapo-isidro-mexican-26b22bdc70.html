Consensus Facts

  • Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as 'El Chapo Isidro,' was captured by Mexican forces in Guasave, Sinaloa state, and flown to Mexico City to be handed over to prosecutors.
  • Meza Flores was one of the FBI's 10 most wanted fugitives and was wanted by the US for trafficking drugs into the country.
  • The United States had offered a reward of $5 million for information leading to his arrest.
  • At least 11 people were killed in the operation: seven suspected cartel gunmen and four Mexican soldiers, according to Mexico's Defense Ministry.
  • Meza Flores climbed cartel ranks to lead the Beltrán Leyva organization and later headed his own group, the Meza-Flores Transnational Criminal Organization.
  • He was a key ally of 'Los Mayos' (the 'Mayos' / Pacific Cartel faction of the Sinaloa Cartel), which the US designated a foreign terrorist organization.
  • Two other high-ranking men were captured alongside him: Germán N. ('Serafín'), the alleged second-in-command, and José N., an alleged financial operator of the organization.
  • The capture set off a wave of violence in Guasave including a firefight, burning trucks and vehicles, roadblocks, and civilians fleeing gunfire, including at a baseball game.
  • The operation involved Mexican army and National Guard special forces, and reporting ties it to US intelligence cooperation.
  • The arrest occurred as President Claudia Sheinbaum faces pressure from US President Donald Trump to crack down on cartels.
  • Mexican Security Secretary Omar García Harfuch said on X that armed forces would remain deployed in Sinaloa to protect the population.
