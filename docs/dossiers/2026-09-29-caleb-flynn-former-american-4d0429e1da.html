Consensus Facts

  • Caleb Flynn, 40, a former 'American Idol' contestant, was found guilty on Tuesday of murdering his wife, Ashley Flynn.
  • The jury convicted Flynn on all nine counts, including murder, assault, and tampering with evidence.
  • The jury deliberated for roughly two hours before reaching the verdict.
  • Flynn faces up to life in prison without the possibility of parole; sentencing was set for October 5.
  • Flynn broke down in tears, wiped his nose and eyes, and was taken from the courtroom in handcuffs after the verdict was read.
  • Ashley Flynn was fatally shot in the couple's home in Tipp City, Ohio, in February (Feb. 16).
  • Flynn called 911 to report that an intruder had broken into their home and shot his wife.
  • Prosecutors alleged Flynn staged the scene to look like an intruder was responsible.
  • The murder weapon (a handgun Flynn said he kept in his truck's center console) was never found.
  • Prosecutors pointed to Flynn's affair with a fellow church figure as motive; his former mistress Alleigha Botner, 24, gave key testimony including reading text messages in which Flynn discussed wanting his wife dead.
  • In an August 2025 text, Flynn wrote he was up until 4 a.m. 'thinking of ways to kill her and not go to jail.'
  • The prosecution called 40 witnesses; the defense rested without calling any witnesses.
  • Assistant Prosecuting Attorney Matthew C. Joseph delivered the closing argument for the state; defense attorney Patrick Mulligan argued that infidelity does not equal murder and that the case was not proven.
  • Flynn appeared on 'American Idol' in 2013 (Season 12) but did not advance past the auditions.
  • Flynn worked as a worship leader / music pastor.
