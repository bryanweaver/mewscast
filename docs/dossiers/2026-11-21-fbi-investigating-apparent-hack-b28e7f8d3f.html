Consensus Facts

  • The FBI is investigating an apparent breach of its National Crime Information Center (NCIC) database (reported by CNN, Axios, and NBC News).
  • The NCIC database connects federal, state, and local law enforcement to share information such as warrants and background checks (CNN, Axios, NBC News).
  • The breach was first reported Friday night, with CNN reporting it first and NBC and Axios following (CNN, Axios, NBC News).
  • The breach claim rests on two people briefed on the matter; the FBI had no comment as of Friday night (CNN, NBC News).
  • The NCIC breach follows a series of recent cybersecurity incidents affecting other agencies, including the USDA, the NSA, and the FAA air traffic control system (CNN, Axios, NBC News).
  • In a Fox News interview Saturday, President Trump downplayed the breach, saying 'it's not good — I don't like that,' while suggesting it might reveal information about what he called the 'Russia Russia Russia hoax' and 'voter fraud' (CNN, Axios).
  • This is not the FBI's first recent breach; a September incident involving the hacking group ShinyHunters targeted agent/employee data via the FBI jobs portal (CNN, NBC News).
