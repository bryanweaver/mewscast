Consensus Facts

  • US President Donald Trump publicly said on Saturday (Sept. 26, 2026) that he rejected Iran's proposal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
  • Trump argued Iran wants a deal 'because they are losing so badly' and framed the offer as evidence of Iranian weakness.
  • Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said no rejection had been conveyed through official channels/mediators, noting Trump makes 'contradictory' statements, and that Iran would await the mediators' definitive response before deciding.
  • Iran's proposal was a seven-day roadmap: within roughly four to five days the US would lift its naval blockade on Iranian ports, waive/lift Iranian oil sanctions, release frozen Iranian assets, and observe a regional ceasefire (including Lebanon and Yemen); the Strait of Hormuz would then reopen and final-deal negotiations would begin around day seven.
  • The new proposal closely mirrors the June memorandum of understanding (MoU) between the US and Iran, which collapsed within days/the following month amid renewed strikes over shipping routes.
  • Qatar has been acting as a mediator, passing messages between the two sides on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York.
  • The US and Israel launched their war on Iran on February 28, 2026, after which Iran effectively closed/blocked the Strait of Hormuz, through which roughly 20% (one-fifth) of the world's oil flowed before the conflict.
  • A US official told Al Jazeera that Washington is in no hurry to reach an agreement, saying the US is in a strong position with control of the Strait of Hormuz.
  • The US recently imposed aviation/secondary sanctions targeting companies doing business with Iranian airlines, prompting flight cancellations affecting neighboring countries; Iraq is seeking exemptions for humanitarian flights.
  • Trump posted an image on Truth Social labeling the waterway the 'Trump Strait.'
  • Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian said Tehran no longer trusts talks with Washington and that the Hormuz issue should be resolved through negotiation rather than force.
  • The proposal called for the US to release an estimated $12bn in frozen Iranian assets.
