Consensus Facts

  • Vivek Ramaswamy, the Republican candidate for Ohio governor, ran an attack ad featuring Amy Acton's estranged birth mother, Donna Arthur, who calls Acton a 'liar'.
  • Ramaswamy's campaign took responsibility for the ad and is spending $4 million to air it across Ohio.
  • Acton, a physician and Ohio's former COVID-19-era state health director, is the Democratic candidate for Ohio governor running against Ramaswamy.
  • Acton has said she was sexually abused as a child by her stepfather (Arthur's husband), who was indicted by a grand jury on a rape charge.
  • Acton and her brother were removed from Arthur's custody after the stepfather's indictment; Acton says she has not seen her mother since 1978 (nearly 50 years).
  • The ad does not mention the abuse history or the circumstances under which Acton lost contact with her mother.
  • Acton's brother, Philip Stearns, released a video corroborating Acton's account of childhood abuse and said their mother stayed married to the alleged abuser until his death.
  • Republican Gov. Mike DeWine, who endorsed Ramaswamy, called the ad 'deeply, deeply troubling' and urged the Ramaswamy campaign to take it down, but did not pull his endorsement.
  • DeWine referenced his experience prosecuting sexual assault cases in explaining why he found the ad wrong given the family backstory.
  • Vice President JD Vance defended Ramaswamy and said he was 'disgusted' by Republicans 'whining' about the ad.
  • Republican Sen. Jon Husted said he 'would not have run that ad'.
  • Nikki Haley called Ramaswamy 'a bad guy' in a social media post.
  • President Donald Trump said it was unusual for a mother to hate a daughter but that it had nothing to do with Vivek.
  • Former Republican Rep. Jim Renacci wrote on X that 'the floor may have just fallen out from under the Republican campaign for Ohio Governor' and urged Republicans to distance themselves.
  • The election is scheduled for November 3, 2026 (the midterm), and if Acton wins she would be the first Democrat elected Ohio governor in two decades.
  • The ad initially circulated on social media without a legally required disclaimer identifying its sponsor.
  • The governor's race is considered highly competitive, with polling showing Acton with a narrow or single-digit lead.
