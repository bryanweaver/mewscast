Consensus Facts

  • The U.S. House of Representatives passed a Russia sanctions bill by a vote of 262-159, sending it to President Trump's desk.
  • The bill is named for the late Sen. Lindsey Graham (R-S.C.), who championed the legislation and died earlier in 2026.
  • The measure had previously passed the Senate by an 86-11 margin in August.
  • The legislation grants the president authority to impose tariffs of up to 100% on top purchasers of Russian oil and gas, such as China and India.
  • The bill targets Russian officials, oligarchs, banks and financial institutions, and Russia's 'shadow fleet' of tankers used to evade sanctions.
  • The vote scrambled usual partisan lines, with some Democrats supporting the bill and some Republicans (particularly the isolationist wing) opposing it.
  • Democratic opposition centered on concerns that the tariff authority could be used by Trump against other countries, including U.S. allies.
  • The legislation was more than a year (or a year and a half) in the making, dating to a sanctions push Graham launched in spring 2025.
  • The White House/Trump administration had held up the bill for months before eventually backing it.
