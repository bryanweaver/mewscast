Consensus Facts

  • Israel marked the third anniversary of the October 7, 2023 Hamas-led attack on October 7, 2026 (ABC News, Taipei Times, Al Jazeera).
  • The 2023 attack killed about 1,200 people in Israel and resulted in 251 people being taken hostage (ABC News, with Taipei Times also describing it as the deadliest/worst attack ever against Israel).
  • More than 74,000 Palestinians have been killed in Gaza since the war began, according to the Gaza Health Ministry (ABC News cites 74,000+; Al Jazeera cites 74,245).
  • A ceasefire took effect roughly a year ago (October 10, 2025) but Israeli strikes have continued in Gaza (ABC News, Taipei Times).
  • Israel carried out new strikes on Gaza on the anniversary; Gaza officials reported multiple Palestinian deaths that day (Al Jazeera reports at least four killed; Taipei Times reports three killed).
  • Commemorations were held at the Nova music festival site, where the attack began at 6:29am, and at Kibbutz Kfar Aza (ABC News, Taipei Times).
  • Israel is holding elections on October 27, the first since the attack, with the vote looming over the anniversary amid divisions over Netanyahu (ABC News, Taipei Times).
  • Family members of Nova victims attended memorials, including a mother who lost a 25-year-old son and played techno music in tribute (ABC News names Anya Davitashili/son Sean; Taipei Times names Anna Davitashvili/son Shaun Davitashvili — same account).
