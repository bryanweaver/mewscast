Consensus Facts

  • The Trump administration posted a proposed rule in the Federal Register on Monday, August 24, 2026, that would impose a fee of $103,265 on H-1B visa petitions (Politico, CNN, Washington Post headline, The Independent).
  • The proposal follows a June 2026 ruling by a federal judge that struck down Trump's earlier $100,000 H-1B fee as an unlawful tax requiring congressional approval (Politico, CNN, The Independent).
  • Trump first introduced the $100,000 H-1B fee in September 2025 via executive/presidential action (Politico, CNN, The Independent).
  • The proposed rule opens a 30-day public comment period and is not yet final (Politico, CNN, The Independent).
  • The administration justifies the fee as a way to recover/fund immigration system costs and to encourage companies to hire American workers (Politico, CNN, The Independent).
  • H-1B annual caps are 65,000 general visas plus 20,000 for holders of advanced degrees (CNN, The Independent; Politico frames the cap as 85,000 total with 20,000 for advanced degrees).
  • Prior to Trump's order, standard H-1B fees were far lower — roughly $2,000 to $5,000 (CNN cites ~$3,000; The Independent cites $2,000-$5,000).

Disagreements

Whether the proposal is 'new' or a move to make the existing fee 'permanent'
Politico: Frames it as a revived proposal after the earlier fee was blocked ('DHS revived the proposal on Monday').
CNN: Frames it as a plan to 'add' a new $103,265 fee after the previous attempt was voided.
The Independent: Frames it as moving to make the fee 'permanent' before a temporary rule expires in September, one year after issuance.
Framing of the annual H-1B cap
Politico: States approvals are 'capped at 85,000 per year, with 20,000 spots for people with advanced degrees.'
CNN: States law allows 65,000 annually plus another 20,000 reserved for advanced-degree holders.
The Independent: States 65,000 general visas annually plus 20,000 for advanced-degree holders.
Petition/registration volume figures
Politico: Nearly 400,000 petitions (most for renewal) approved in FY2024; caps at 85,000.
The Independent: About 344,000 H-1B registrations last year, down over 25% from 2024 and less than half the 794,000 in 2023.
Prior standard fee amount
CNN: The fee was previously about $3,000.
The Independent: Standard fees ranged between $2,000 and $5,000.
