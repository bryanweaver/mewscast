Consensus Facts

  • Colin Gray, 55, the father of the Apalachee High School shooter, was sentenced to 15 years in prison on Thursday, July 30, 2026, by Judge Nicholas Primm in Barrow County Superior Court in Winder, Georgia.
  • A jury convicted Colin Gray in March 2026 on charges including second-degree murder and involuntary manslaughter.
  • Colin Gray provided/gifted his son the AR-style assault rifle used in the September 4, 2024, shooting at Apalachee High School.
  • The shooting killed four people: students Mason Schermerhorn and Christian Angulo (both 14) and teachers Richard 'Ricky' Aspinwall (39) and Cristina Irimie (53).
  • Colin Gray's son, Colt Gray, was 14 at the time of the attack, is now 16, was charged as an adult, pleaded guilty, and was sentenced to life in prison without the possibility of parole earlier that same week (Tuesday, July 28, 2026).
  • Prosecutors sought the maximum sentence (reported as 80 years), while the defense argued for a lighter sentence.
  • Judge Primm told Gray it was 'clear you failed as a parent' and that 'the warning lights were flashing brighter and brighter,' citing failures to get his son counseling and to secure/remove the guns.
  • Law enforcement had questioned the teenager roughly seven months before the Christmas gift about online threats to commit a school shooting.
  • Victims' family members testified at the hearing and asked the judge to impose the maximum sentence.
  • Sentences on the various convictions were ordered to run concurrently.
  • Colin Gray's case is among the first in the US in which a parent has been held criminally responsible for a mass shooting carried out by their child.
