Consensus Facts

  • A Harris County (Texas) grand jury indicted ICE officer Mario Alberto Morales on a charge of aggravated assault with a deadly weapon, announced Friday, Oct. 30, 2026.
  • The charge stems from an Oct. 10, 2026 incident in which Morales struck Miguel Parra-Hernandez with his vehicle during an enforcement pursuit in Houston.
  • Parra-Hernandez suffered serious injuries, including a broken/fractured leg, and was hospitalized.
  • Harris County District Attorney Sean Teare announced the indictment and played video (described as body camera footage) at a news conference Friday.
  • In the footage, the officer is heard saying words to the effect of 'I was coming in and I hit him with the car.'
  • In an initial statement after the incident, ICE said Parra-Hernandez ran in front of the ICE vehicle; the DA, after reviewing video, accused the agent of deliberately/aggressively driving into him.
