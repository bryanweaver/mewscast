Consensus Facts

  • Iran-backed Houthi (Ansar Allah) forces seized the Yemeni Red Sea port city of Mocha on Thursday, September 10, 2026 (reported by CNN and Al Jazeera).
  • Internationally recognized government forces, led/commanded in this theater by Tariq (Tarik) Saleh, withdrew from Mocha; Saleh described it as a 'tactical withdrawal' (CNN and Al Jazeera).
  • The seizure expands Houthi influence over the Bab al-Mandeb Strait, a key global shipping chokepoint linking the Red Sea, Gulf of Aden and the Suez Canal route between Asia and Europe (CNN and Al Jazeera).
  • After Mocha, the Houthis were advancing/moving south toward Dhubab and other coastal areas (CNN and Al Jazeera).
  • The fighting has driven significant civilian displacement and worsened Yemen's humanitarian crisis; Al Jazeera reports hundreds fleeing Mocha toward Lahij, Aden and Dhubab (CNN references the worsening humanitarian crisis; Al Jazeera details displacement).
