Consensus Facts

  • U.S. envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Russian President Vladimir Putin at the Kremlin in Moscow on Saturday, Sept. 5, 2026.
  • The Moscow meeting lasted more than three hours behind closed doors and ended without any announced breakthroughs.
  • Putin's foreign policy adviser Yuri Ushakov described the talks as constructive and frank.
  • Ushakov said the talks were not limited to Ukraine, and that economic issues and potentially large mutually beneficial Russian-American joint projects were discussed in considerable detail.
  • Witkoff and Kushner traveled to Kyiv on Sunday, Sept. 6, 2026, in their first visit to Ukraine as part of the peace process.
  • Putin and Zelenskyy agreed to pause strikes on each other's capitals for three days while the talks took place.
  • Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Ukraine was ready for a constructive discussion, needed security guarantees and a just postwar peace, and said its proposals were prepared.
  • The last known Moscow visit by Witkoff and Kushner was in January, when they also met Putin.
  • Washington's push to end the fighting had lost momentum, with U.S. attention focused for the past six months on the Iran war.
  • Both sides continued to escalate and exchange aerial attacks around the talks, with Russia launching 108 long-range drones and cruise missiles overnight, of which Ukraine downed 82 drones and six missiles.
  • The war began with Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine on Feb. 24, 2022, and the front line stretches roughly 1,250 kilometers (775 miles).
