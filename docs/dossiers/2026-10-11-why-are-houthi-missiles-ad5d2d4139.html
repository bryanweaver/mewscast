Consensus Facts

  • A missile attack on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport on Saturday, October 11, 2026, killed at least 12 people and wounded roughly 300-309 others (Al Jazeera, CNBC, CNN, CBS News).
  • It was described as the deadliest attack on Saudi soil in more than a decade (Al Jazeera, CBS News).
  • The dead included four Saudi citizens, two Bangladeshis, and one person each from the United States, Jordan, Palestine, Syria, Sudan and Egypt (Al Jazeera, CBS News).
  • The attack is attributed to Yemen's Iran-backed Houthi group (Al Jazeera, CNBC, CNN, CBS News).
  • US President Donald Trump described the attack as 'terrible' and said he is considering/'going to look at' joining Saudi Arabia in the fight against the Houthis, saying the US would 'move very quickly' (Al Jazeera, CNBC, CBS News).
  • US Secretary of State Marco Rubio condemned the attack, noted it killed an American, and said the US stands with Saudi Arabia (Al Jazeera, CBS News).
  • The UN condemned the attack; Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said he was 'deeply shocked' (Al Jazeera, CBS News).
  • Earlier the same week, Houthi attacks on the Riyadh airport killed three Saudis, including a pilot (CNBC, CBS News).
  • The Houthis issued warnings against using Saudi airports and airspace, exempting the holy cities of Mecca and Medina, calling the airports a 'theatre of operations' (CNBC, CBS News).
  • The attacks came in the week after a Saudi-led coalition launched a major military offensive against the Houthis to support Yemeni government forces (Al Jazeera, CNN, CBS News).
  • A passenger terminal at the Riyadh airport (terminals 3 and 4 complex for domestic flights) was struck again on Sunday, the day after the deadly Saturday strike (CNBC, CBS News).
