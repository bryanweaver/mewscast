Consensus Facts

  • The Supreme Court rejected the Trump administration's push to restrict mail-in voting for the November 2026 midterm elections, issuing the order late Monday, Sept. 14.
  • On Tuesday, Sept. 15, Trump publicly attacked the Supreme Court over the ruling in a lengthy social media post.
  • Trump wrote that 'The Court's inability and unwillingness to do the right thing for our Country will go down, in a very negative way, in the annals of History,' and said the court 'is bullied and cajoled by the Radical Left.'
  • The Trump administration had sought to have the U.S. Postal Service play a central role in screening or tightening mail-in ballots.
  • Justices Clarence Thomas and Samuel Alito dissented from the order.
  • Justice Brett Kavanaugh wrote that making the requested changes so close to the midterms would be 'arbitrary and capricious.'
  • The court's majority indicated the administration was unlikely to succeed on the merits of its challenge.
  • Roughly 30% (about a third) of voters cast ballots by mail, and mail ballots have already gone out to voters in some states.
  • Claims of widespread mail-in voting fraud have been repeatedly debunked.
