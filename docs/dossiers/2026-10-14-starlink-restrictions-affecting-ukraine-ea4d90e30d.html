Consensus Facts

  • Ukrainian officials, led by presidential adviser Serhiy Beskrestnov, said SpaceX imposed overnight speed restrictions on Starlink/Starshield terminals that disrupted a number of military missions (BBC, Politico, NBC News, CNN).
  • The restriction causes terminals to lose connection when a vehicle or asset exceeds roughly 100 mph / 160 km/h (BBC, CNN).
  • Ukrainian officials said they received no prior warning of the restriction from SpaceX (BBC, NBC News, CNN).
  • Beskrestnov said the move 'cannot be a coincidence' and is 'linked to the current political situation' (BBC, NBC News, CNN).
  • The disruption particularly affects interceptor drones and mid-range strike drones, as well as front-line logistics, reconnaissance and communications/signal relaying (BBC, NBC News, CNN).
  • SpaceX had not commented or responded to Ukraine as of the reporting (BBC, NBC News, CNN).
  • The Starlink issue occurred alongside a major overnight Russian strike that left about 600,000 people without power (BBC, Politico, CNN).
  • Russia launched 39 ballistic missiles and more than 120 drones at Kyiv, the surrounding region and Kharkiv, targeting energy infrastructure (BBC, Politico, CNN).
  • Zelensky said the strikes hit energy infrastructure covered by the 'energy ceasefire' that President Trump announced the prior weekend (BBC, Politico, CNN).
  • The Starlink disruption coincided with a dispute over US intelligence sharing, with Trump denying the US had stopped sharing intel while Ukraine said it received no early warning of the strikes (BBC, NBC News, CNN).
