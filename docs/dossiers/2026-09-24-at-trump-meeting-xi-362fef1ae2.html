Consensus Facts

  • President Donald Trump welcomed Chinese President Xi Jinping to Washington for a high-stakes state visit, with Xi arriving at Joint Base Andrews on Wednesday, Sept. 23, 2026.
  • Trump personally greeted Xi on the tarmac at Joint Base Andrews — a rare step for a sitting US president; both outlets note it was the first such airport greeting in roughly a decade or 11 years.
  • The arrival ceremony included a military band, a red carpet, and a bomber flyover (Al Jazeera cites a single B-1; CBS cites two B-1 bombers), with both leaders' wives present (Xi's wife, Peng Liyuan).
  • Xi issued a written arrival statement saying China and the United States 'should be partners, not rivals.'
  • Trump and Xi last met in May, when Xi hosted Trump for a visit in Beijing.
  • Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent met with Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng ahead of the visit and discussed the possibility of a 'bigger deal' beyond the existing tariff truce.
  • The existing tariff truce (referred to as the Busan Agreement/arrangement, struck in Busan, South Korea, in October) was set to expire Nov. 10, 2025.
  • Key issues expected to be discussed include trade/tariffs, AI/technology, and Taiwan.
  • The visit schedule includes a White House arrival ceremony, a formal meeting of the two presidents, and a state dinner on Thursday, followed by a visit to the National Archives.
