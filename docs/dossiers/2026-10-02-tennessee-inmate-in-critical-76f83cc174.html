Consensus Facts

  • Christa Gail Pike, 50, is in critical condition and receiving lifesaving medical care at a hospital after Tennessee's failed attempt to execute her.
  • Pike survived two doses of the lethal drug pentobarbital administered on Wednesday, Sept. 30, 2026, and remained alive, prompting transport to a hospital by ambulance.
  • Pike was sentenced to death for the 1995 murder of 19-year-old Colleen Slemmer, a crime she committed at age 18; she was convicted in 1996.
  • She would have been the first woman executed in Tennessee in more than 200 years.
  • Attorney Randy Spivey described the execution attempt as 'cruel and torturous' and said at least seven needles were used in Pike's left arm over roughly an hour, with one needle appearing bent when pulled out.
  • Spivey said Pike was cooperative, telling the execution team where to find a vein, saying 'please try here' and asking them to try higher on her shoulder.
  • Witnesses reported Pike was still breathing, snoring, moaning and moving after the drugs were administered, including after the chamber curtain was closed.
  • Republican Gov. Bill Lee ordered an independent/third-party review and indefinitely halted/paused the remaining scheduled executions for the year, calling the event a tragedy and saying it 'shouldn't have happened.'
  • Pike's lawyers are calling on Gov. Lee to commute her death sentence to life in prison, a request he had previously denied.
  • The Tennessee Department of Correction stated it appeared to have followed its lawful, established execution protocol.
  • This is the second failed execution attempt in Tennessee in 2026; in May, the execution of Tony Carruthers was called off when staff could not find a vein, and he received a one-year reprieve.
  • The execution pause will affect the December execution of Gary (Wayne) Sutton, a convicted murderer.
  • May Martinez, Slemmer's mother, who favored the execution and traveled to witness it, expressed that she did not get justice and described a disturbing scene.
  • Pike's attempted execution came after last-minute legal challenges, including appeals that reached the courts.
