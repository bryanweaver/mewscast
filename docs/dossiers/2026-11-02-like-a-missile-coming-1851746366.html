Consensus Facts

  • A Learjet 60 carrying nine people crashed in Palm Beach County, Florida, on Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, and all nine on board were killed (NBC News, AP, CNN; corroborated in Al Jazeera's body).
  • The jet had departed Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas and was bound for the Bahamas (NBC News, AP, CNN, Al Jazeera).
  • Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy confirmed the deaths and called the crash a 'horrific tragedy' (NBC News, AP, CNN).
  • Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis described the scene, saying 'It looked like a missile going down' and that it left a big crater in the road (NBC News, AP, CNN).
  • DeSantis/officials said there were no reports of anyone on the ground being killed or injured, though the crash created a significant debris field (NBC News, AP, CNN).
  • The crash occurred around 12:50 p.m. and the plane nose-dived before erupting into a fireball, leaving a crater in the roadway (NBC News, AP, CNN).
  • Minutes before the crash, the pilot reported 'light, clear ice that is manageable' and moderate turbulence, then later reported precipitation and requested to climb (NBC News, AP, CNN).
  • The pilot declared an emergency and radioed mayday calls describing descent of 10,000 then 20,000 feet per minute, and said 'We're going to crash into the ground' (NBC News, AP, CNN).
  • A flood watch / storms were in effect for southern Florida at the time of the crash (NBC News, AP, CNN).
  • The aircraft is registered to an LLC in Delaware per FAA records (NBC News, CNN).
  • The FAA and NTSB will investigate, with NTSB investigators arriving Sunday and Monday and a Monday news briefing planned in West Palm Beach (AP, CNN).
  • About 600 people near the crash site (a mobile home community) needed to be sheltered/evacuated because the scene blocked access (AP, CNN).
  • The identities of those on board had not been released at the time of reporting (NBC News, CNN).
