Consensus Facts

  • A 41-year-old senior U.S. Army serviceman was arrested near Gloucester, England, on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, on suspicion of espionage for Russia.
  • The arrest was made by British Counter Terrorism Policing under Section 1 (obtaining or disclosing protected information) and Section 3 (assisting a foreign intelligence service) of the National Security Act 2023.
  • The suspect remains in custody at a police station in London and was unnamed in all reports.
  • Officers are carrying out searches at two locations in Gloucestershire as part of the investigation.

  • The National Security Act 2023 allows police to hold the suspect without charge for up to 14 days.
