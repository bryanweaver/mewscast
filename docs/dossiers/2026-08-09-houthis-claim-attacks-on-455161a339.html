Consensus Facts

  • Yemen's Houthis claimed responsibility for a drone attack on a Saudi Aramco refinery in Jazan/Jizan, southwestern Saudi Arabia, on Sunday, Aug. 9, 2026 (AP, NPR, CNBC).
  • Saudi Arabia's Energy Ministry said a fire at the Aramco refinery was extinguished with no injuries and did not state the cause (AP, NPR, CNBC).
  • The Houthis said the strike was in response to Saudi drone incursions into Yemeni airspace (NPR, CNBC).
  • Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Iran is not in direct talks with the U.S., but that messages are being exchanged through intermediaries (NPR, CNBC).
  • Araghchi said an agreement with Oman on transit routes does not mean the Strait of Hormuz will reopen, which depends on additional conditions conveyed through intermediaries (NPR, CNBC).
  • Iran's Supreme National Security Council secretary Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr laid out sweeping demands for reopening the strait: lifting the naval blockade and sanctions, U.S. military withdrawal from the region, war reparations, release of frozen assets, and an end to attacks on Iran's allies (NPR, CNBC).
  • Under a June interim/memorandum-of-understanding deal, a schedule to end sanctions, a compensation plan, and negotiations over frozen assets would be part of a final deal (NPR, CNBC).
  • Iran and Oman are working on a temporary arrangement in which inbound traffic transits Iranian waters and outbound traffic transits Omani waters (NPR, CNBC).
  • On Thursday, Houthi attacks in Marib and Hadramout provinces killed at least 30 government troops, and separate Houthi attacks in southern Saudi Arabia wounded 11 civilians, including a young child (Al Jazeera, AP Article 7).
  • The escalation is described as the most significant since the April/2022 truce that halted major fighting between the Houthis and the Saudi-led coalition (AP, NPR, Al Jazeera, AP Article 7).

It states the Mocha port attack and resulting deaths as established fact without in-line attribution. Article 7 is a separate, more granular piece leading on the human toll — a 4-year-old child injured — and foregrounds the Saudi military spokesman's characterization of 'terrorist' attacks and 'flagrant violation' of humanitarian law, while noting the Houthis gave no statement.
