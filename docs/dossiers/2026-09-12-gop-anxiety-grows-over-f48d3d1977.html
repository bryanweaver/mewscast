Consensus Facts

  • The North Carolina Senate race pits Republican nominee Michael Whatley, former chairman of the RNC and the North Carolina GOP, against Democrat Roy Cooper, former North Carolina governor.
  • The seat is currently held by Republican Sen. Thom Tillis, who is not seeking reelection.
  • A new Elon University poll released Thursday found Cooper leading Whatley by 11 points (49% to 38%) among likely voters.
  • The race is widely viewed as one of Democrats' top pickup/flip opportunities in the 2026 midterms and could help decide control of the Senate.
  • Cost of living and inflation are voters' top concerns heading into the election.
  • President Trump handpicked Whatley to lead the RNC before Whatley stepped down to run for Senate at Trump's urging.
  • Whatley spoke at the Republican convention in Dallas in September 2026.
  • Christopher (Chris) Cooper, a political science professor at Western Carolina University (not related to Roy Cooper), commented on the race's dynamics.
  • North Carolina last elected a Democratic U.S. senator in 2008, and Trump has carried the state in recent presidential elections.
