Consensus Facts

  • A FlyDubai flight (Flight FZ1073) traveling from Dubai to Tel Aviv on Wednesday, Sept. 30, 2026, was diverted and made an emergency landing at Tabuk, Saudi Arabia (Prince Sultan bin Abdulaziz International Airport).
  • Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said one pilot stabbed the other pilot and apparently tried to crash the plane; this is attributed to Israeli officials, not independently confirmed.
  • Passengers and crew intervened to subdue the attacking pilot, and the plane landed safely; Netanyahu credited a passenger and a crew member with breaking into the cockpit and another crew member with stabilizing the aircraft.
  • The aircraft descended rapidly during the incident (reported ranges include from 33,000/34,000 feet down to 16,000/17,000 feet).
  • Saudi authorities arrested the pilot and were questioning him; his identity was not immediately shared.
  • The passengers were predominantly Israeli citizens (Netanyahu's office said around 160 of the roughly 170 aboard were Israeli, per AP).
  • Israel scrambled warplanes/fighter jets amid concerns about a possible hijacking.
  • The flight transmitted emergency signals, including a squawk code indicating a possible hijacking or unlawful interference.
  • Israeli officials, including Defense Minister Israel Katz, characterized the incident as an attempted terror attack.
