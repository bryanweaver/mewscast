NBC carefully hedges: it states it could not access or independently authenticate the data, notes Russia's last official figure was ~6,000 (Shoigu, early in the war), notes the prior verified named list stood at ~289,000, and cites a senior British defense official's September estimate of roughly half a million. NBC foregrounds methodology and verification steps (95% cross-match with named list, probate registry checks, vital-record-number anti-tampering checks).
