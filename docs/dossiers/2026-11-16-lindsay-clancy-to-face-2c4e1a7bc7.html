Consensus Facts

  • A judge set a new trial date of March 29, 2027, for Lindsay Clancy, whose first trial for the deaths of her three children ended in a deadlocked jury (NYT and BBC body text).
  • Judge William Sullivan made the scheduling decision at a hearing on Monday, November 16, 2026 (NYT and BBC).
  • Lindsay Clancy is 36 years old (NYT and BBC).
  • The three children were Cora, 5; Dawson, 3; and Callan, 8 months (NYT and BBC).
  • Prosecutors have not explicitly confirmed whether they will retry Clancy on first-degree murder charges, lesser charges, or at all (NYT and BBC).
  • Defense lawyer Kevin Reddington said the DA's request for a jury trial indicated an intent to proceed on first-degree murder charges, saying one can 'read between the lines' (NYT and BBC).
  • The first trial's jury deadlocked 11-to-1, with the majority inclined toward a not-guilty-by-reason-of-insanity verdict, and a mistrial was declared in September (NYT and BBC).
  • The defense argued Clancy suffered from severe postpartum mental illness/psychosis (NYT and BBC).
  • Judge Sullivan previously rejected a defense motion to dismiss the case on double jeopardy grounds (NYT and BBC).
  • Clancy is currently held in a state-run psychiatric hospital/medical facility in Massachusetts (NYT and BBC).
  • The case sparked a national debate over how postpartum mental illness is treated in the US courts (NYT and BBC).
