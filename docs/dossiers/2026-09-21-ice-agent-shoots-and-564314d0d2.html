Consensus Facts

  • An ICE officer shot and wounded a man in Austin, Texas, on Sunday afternoon, September 20, 2026, on the city's north side.
  • The man was shot in the torso and was in serious but stable condition at a hospital, per Austin-Travis County EMS Chief Robert Luckritz.
  • The man was identified by immigration attorney Kate Lincoln-Goldfinch as Wilber Rafael Garces Perez (also spelled Garcés Perez), a 28-year-old Venezuelan.
  • The shooting occurred around 1 p.m. following a traffic stop / vehicle pursuit involving a blue Toyota Corolla, and involved a foot chase or 'small foot pursuit' per Austin Police Chief Lisa Davis.
  • Austin Police Department was not involved in the ICE operation or the shooting, per Mayor Kirk Watson and Chief Davis.
  • The Texas Department of Public Safety received a request from ICE for assistance in a vehicle pursuit, but troopers arrived after the shooting had already occurred and then assisted with medical treatment.
  • The Department of Homeland Security, which oversees ICE, did not respond to requests for comment and did not address the incident on its social media accounts.
  • Austin Mayor Kirk Watson, a Democrat, expressed anger and skepticism about ICE conducting its own investigation, and stated he wants local/third-party involvement in the investigation.
  • Democratic Congressman Greg Casar, who represents parts of Austin, called for transparency and an independent investigation and criticized the ICE surge in the city.
  • The man's family and lawyer said they were unable to get information from the hospital about his condition or location.
  • A crowd of protesters (roughly 100, per Al Jazeera) gathered at the scene with anti-ICE signs.
  • The shooting comes amid a nationwide surge in ICE enforcement during the Trump administration.
