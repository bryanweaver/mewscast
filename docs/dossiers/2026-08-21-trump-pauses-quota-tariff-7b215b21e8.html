Consensus Facts

  • President Trump announced on Friday (Aug. 21, 2026) via Truth Social that the U.S. would waive/exempt higher tariffs on up to 300,000 metric tons of imported ground beef.
  • Trump stated there is a commitment that this beef will be sold at 25 percent below current market prices.

  • The stated purpose of the measure is to lower historically high beef prices for consumers.
