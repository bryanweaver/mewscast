Consensus Facts

  • A federal judge, US District Judge Ana C. Reyes, temporarily blocked the Trump administration from closing East Potomac Park and its golf course in Washington, DC, on Saturday.
  • Trump had announced the course/park would close on Sunday, November 15, ahead of planned renovations into a championship golf course.
  • Judge Reyes issued a 30-page order finding the administration's stated reasons for the closure were 'pretextual.'
  • The order keeps the park and golf course open and accessible to the public; NYT states it lasts two weeks, BBC states until November 28.
  • The administration is not required to actively manage or operate the course while the case proceeds.
  • The litigation began in February when a preservationist organization (DC Preservation League) and local golfers sued over the redevelopment plans.
  • In October, the National Park Service removed hundreds of trees (over 400 per CNN) at the site, prompting Reyes to block further tree removal and impose notice requirements.
  • Reyes has opened an inquiry, aided by a special master, into whether Justice Department/Interior officials made misrepresentations or withheld information from the court.
  • Trump has publicly disparaged the existing course, calling it 'Trash Island' and among the worst in the world, while promoting the renovation.
  • Tom Fazio, a golf designer who worked on Trump Organization courses, is associated with the proposed redesign.
